# NHTSA recall 23V156000: Power Train:Automatic Transmission:Control Module (Tcm/Pcm/Tecm)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V156000 |
| Report received | 2023-03-23 |
| Manufacturer | Spartan Fire, LLC |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:CONTROL MODULE (TCM/PCM/TECM) |
| Potential units affected | 166 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

KME redundant PANTHER 2022, KME redundant PANTHER 2023, KME redundant PANTHER 2024, SPARTAN FIRE FC-94 2022, SPARTAN FIRE FC-94 2023, SPARTAN FIRE FC-94 2024, SPARTAN FIRE GLADIATOR 2022, SPARTAN FIRE GLADIATOR 2023, SPARTAN FIRE GLADIATOR 2024, SPARTAN FIRE METROSTAR 2022, SPARTAN FIRE METROSTAR 2023, SPARTAN FIRE METROSTAR 2024

## Defect

Spartan Fire LLC (Spartan Fire) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Gladiator, Metro Star, FC-94 and KME Panther firetrucks.  After the vehicle is reversed, the transmission control module (TCM) may fail to achieve the 4th gear lockup needed to initiate the water pump.

## Consequence

A firetruck that is unable to pump water increases the risk of injury during a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the TCM software, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 27, 2023.  Owners may contact Spartan Fire customer service at 1-517-543-6400.  Spartan Fire's number for this recall is 23007.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
