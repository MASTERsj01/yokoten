# NHTSA recall 24V383000: Power Train:Axle Hubs

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V383000 |
| Report received | 2024-05-30 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AXLE HUBS |
| Potential units affected | 1374 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR LEGEND 2023, AUTOCAR LEGEND 2024, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2023, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2024

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling certain 2023-2024 ACX (Xpeditor) and DC (Legend) vehicles.  The axle lock nut may be improperly secured, allowing the wheel end to separate.

## Consequence

The wheel end separating from the vehicle increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and secure or replace the lock nut, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 26, 2024.  Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611.  Autocar's numbers for this recall are ACX-2402 and DC-2405.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
