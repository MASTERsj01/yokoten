# NHTSA recall 23V209000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V209000 |
| Report received | 2023-09-13 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 380 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2021, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2022, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2023

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Thomas Built Bus SAF-TLINER C2 JOULEY school buses equipped with an automatic low voltage disconnect switch.  The automatic low voltage disconnect switch may fail, resulting in a loss of drive power, power steering, or vehicle lighting.

## Consequence

A loss of drive power, power steering, or vehicle lighting can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will remove the automatic low voltage disconnect switch, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 18, 2023.  Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712.  DTNA's number for this recall is FL968.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
