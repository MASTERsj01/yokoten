# NHTSA recall 21V551000: Steering:Linkages:Tie Rod Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V551000 |
| Report received | 2021-07-21 |
| Manufacturer | Shyft Group |
| Component | STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 76 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

UTILIMASTER WALK-IN VAN 2021, UTILIMASTER WALK-IN VAN 2022

## Defect

Shyft Group is recalling certain 2021-2022 Utilimaster Walk-In vans.  The tie rod clamps may be loose, which could result in loose tie rod ends that could break or detach.

## Consequence

Detached or broken tie rod ends can result in steering loss, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Daimler Trucks North America (DTNA) will mail owner letters on behalf of Shyft Group, and DTNA service facilities will replace the tie rod clamp bolts and nuts, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on December 23, 2021.  Owners may contact Shyft Group customer service at 1-800-582-3454.  Shyft Group's number for this recall is 21-17.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
