# NHTSA recall 21V319000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V319000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-05 |
| Manufacturer | Shyft Group |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 2783 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SPARTAN K2 2017, SPARTAN K2 2018, SPARTAN K2 2019, SPARTAN K2 2020, SPARTAN K2 2021, SPARTAN K2 2022, SPARTAN K3 2017, SPARTAN K3 2018, SPARTAN K3 2019, SPARTAN K3 2020, SPARTAN K3 2021, SPARTAN K3 2022

## Defect

Shyft Group is recalling certain 2017-2022 Spartan RV Chassis K2 and K3 vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the rail threads and fuel lines, and replace the rail as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on August 25, 2021. Owners may contact Shyft Group customer service at 1-800-543-4277.  Shyft Group's number for this recall is 21-11.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
