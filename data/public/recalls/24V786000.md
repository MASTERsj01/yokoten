# NHTSA recall 24V786000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V786000 |
| Report received | 2024-10-21 |
| Manufacturer | Brinkley RV |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 1319 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BRINKLEY RV MODEL G 2024, BRINKLEY RV MODEL G 2025

## Defect

Brinkley RV (Brinkley) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Model G fifth wheel trailers.  The solar panels may have been incorrectly wired, which can overload the circuits and cause the wiring to overheat.

## Consequence

Overheated wires increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will rewire the solar power system, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 29, 2024.  Owners may contact Brinkley customer service at 1-574-501-4280.  Brinkley's number for this recall is REC-2404.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
