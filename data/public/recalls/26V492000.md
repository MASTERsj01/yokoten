# NHTSA recall 26V492000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V492000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-28 |
| Manufacturer | Grand Design RV, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 7 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

GRAND DESIGN IMAGINE 2027

## Defect

Grand Design RV, LLC (Grand Design) is recalling certain 2027 Imagine recreational trailers. The inverter prep circuit conductor may have insufficient overcurrent protection, causing the conductor to overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated conductor may increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the breaker, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 17, 2026. Owners may contact Grand Design customer service at 1-574-825-9679. Grand Design's number for this recall is 910066.
