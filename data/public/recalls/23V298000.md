## Vehicles

KIA NIRO 2023, KIA NIRO PHEV 2023, KIA SOUL 2023, KIA SPORTAGE 2023, KIA SPORTAGE HYBRID 2023, KIA SPORTAGE PHEV 2023, KIA TELLURIDE 2023

## Defect

Kia America, Inc. (Kia) is recalling certain 2023 Sportage, Sportage Hybrid, Sportage Plug-in Hybrid, Niro Hybrid, Niro Plug-in Hybrid, Soul, and Telluride vehicles equipped with a digital instrument cluster containing a 4.2" LCD screen.Â Â The instrument cluster may fail to display any information when the vehicle is started.Â Â As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Control and Displays," and 138, "Tire Pressure Monitoring Systems."

## Consequence

A blank instrument cluster display will not show critical information, such as tire pressure, warning messages, and warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the instrument cluster software, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 26, 2023.  Owners may contact Kia customer service at 1-800-333-4542.  Kia's number for this recall is SC270.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
