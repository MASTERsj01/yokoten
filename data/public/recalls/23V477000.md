# NHTSA recall 23V477000: Visibility:Windshield Wiper/Washer:Linkages

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V477000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-12 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:LINKAGES |
| Potential units affected | 1205 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ACCOLADE 2021, ENTEGRA ACCOLADE 2022, ENTEGRA ACCOLADE XL 2021, ENTEGRA ACCOLADE XL 2022, JAYCO SENECA 2021, JAYCO SENECA 2022, JAYCO SENECA PRESTIGE 2021, JAYCO SENECA PRESTIGE 2022

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Entegra Coach Accolade, Accolade XL, Jayco Seneca, and Seneca Prestige recreational vehicles.  The windshield wipers may fail, resulting in reduced driver visibility.

## Consequence

Reduced driver visibility increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the windshield wiper arms, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 11, 2023.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
