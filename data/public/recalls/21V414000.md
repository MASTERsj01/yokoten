# NHTSA recall 21V414000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V414000 |
| Report received | 2021-06-02 |
| Manufacturer | Volvo Car USA, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS; FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP |
| Potential units affected | 85550 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

VOLVO S60 2019, VOLVO S60 2020, VOLVO S90 2019, VOLVO S90 2020, VOLVO V60 2019, VOLVO V60 2020, VOLVO V60CC 2019, VOLVO V60CC 2020, VOLVO V90 2019, VOLVO V90 2020, VOLVO V90CC 2019, VOLVO V90CC 2020 and others

## Defect

Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2019-2020 V90, XC60, S60, V60, V60CC, S90, V90CC, S90L, and XC90 vehicles.  The low-pressure fuel pump can blow a fuse and fail.

## Consequence

Failure of the low-pressure fuel pump can cause a stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuse, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 2, 2021.  Owners may contact Volvo Car customer service at 1-888-458-1552.  Volvo Car's number for this recall is R10100.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
