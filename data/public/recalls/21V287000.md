# NHTSA recall 21V287000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V287000 |
| Report received | 2021-04-23 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 88 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

COACHMEN CATALINA 2021

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling 2021 Coachment Catalina travel trailers.  A wire with the incorrect gauge was routed from the battery and may melt.

## Consequence

A melted wire could cause a short, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Forest River will notify owners, and dealers will replace the wire, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 13, 2021.  Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-825-8657.  Forest River's number for this recall is 203-1327.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
