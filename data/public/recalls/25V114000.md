# NHTSA recall 25V114000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V114000 |
| Report received | 2025-02-21 |
| Manufacturer | Shyft Group |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 85 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SPARTAN K2 2022, SPARTAN K2 2023, SPARTAN K2 2024, SPARTAN K2 2025

## Defect

Shyft Group (Shyft) is recalling certain 2022-2025 Spartan RV Chassis K2 vehicles equipped with Cummins L9 diesel engines.  The remote fuel filter hose may separate from the fitting, causing a fuel leak and an engine stall.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.  An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Shyft will work with the equipment manufacturer, Cummins, to replace the fuel hoses, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed March 10, 2025.  Owners may contact RV Chassis customer service at 855-589-9836.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 888-327-4236 (TTY 888-275-9171) or go to nhtsa.gov.
