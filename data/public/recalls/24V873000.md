# NHTSA recall 24V873000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Cooling System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V873000 |
| Report received | 2024-11-19 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 758 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2022, NOVA BUS LFS 2023, NOVA BUS LFS 2024, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2023

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova Bus) is recalling certain 2023 LFS Artic and 2022-2024 LFS buses.  The coolant sensor could send a false signal, causing the engine to shut down.

## Consequence

An unexpected engine shut down can increase the risk of a crash.  Additionally, transferring passengers from the disabled bus can increase the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Nova Bus will inspect and replace the coolant sensor as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 20, 2025.  Owners may contact Nova Bus customer service at 1-800-350-6682.  Nova Bus's number for this recall is CR5668.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
