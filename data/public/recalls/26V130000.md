# NHTSA recall 26V130000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V130000 |
| Report received | 2026-03-05 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 116 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER IMPRESSION 2026

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2026 Impression fifth wheel recreational trailers. The insulation on the wires may have been improperly removed, exposing conductors.

## Consequence

Exposed conductors may cause electrical arcing, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and correct the wiring as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 17, 2026. Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-327-2716. Forest River's number for this recall is 83-2039.
