# NHTSA recall 25V875000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V875000 |
| Report received | 2025-12-17 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 1 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR E-ACTT 2024

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling one 2024 E-ACTT terminal tractor. The battery cable connections at the traction motors and the power inverter may not be properly secured.

## Consequence

Improperly secured cable connections may overheat, increasing the risk of a fire. Additionally loose connections may cause a loss of motive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Autocar will inspect and properly secure the cable connections as necessary, free of charge. All vehicles were repaired by February 4, 2026. Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611. Autocar's number for this recall is ACTT-2505.
