# NHTSA recall 21V174000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V174000 |
| Report received | 2021-03-12 |
| Manufacturer | ARBOC Specialty Vehicles, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 29 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ARBOC SPECIALTY VEHICLES SPIRIT OF FREEDOM 2021

## Defect

ARBOC Specialty Vehicles, LLC (ARBOC) is recalling certain 2021 Spirit of Freedom Ford E450 vehicles.  The fuel hose may not be secured correctly, which can result in a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of fire.

## Remedy

ARBOC has notified owners, and dealers will inspect and repair the fuel hose connection, free of charge.  The recall began March 16, 2021.  Owners may contact ARBOC customer service at 1-866-953-5555.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
