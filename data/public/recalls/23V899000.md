# NHTSA recall 23V899000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V899000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-26 |
| Manufacturer | Mack Trucks, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 284 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MACK ANTHEM (AN) 2024, MACK PINNACLE (PI/PN) 2024, MACK TERRAPRO (TE) 2024

## Defect

Mack Trucks, Inc. (Mack) is recalling certain 2024 Anthem, Pinnacle, and Terrapro vehicles.  The steering gear assemblies may have been assembled incorrectly with fewer recirculating balls than are required, which may result in a loss of steering control.

## Consequence

A loss of steering control increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the defective gears as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 9, 2024.  Owners may contact Mack customer service at 1-800-866-1177.  Mack's number for this recall is SC0457.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
