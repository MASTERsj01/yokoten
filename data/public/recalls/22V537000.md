# NHTSA recall 22V537000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V537000 |
| Report received | 2022-07-26 |
| Manufacturer | Capacity of Texas |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 58 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CAPACITY TJ 5000 2021, CAPACITY TJ 5000 2022, CAPACITY TJ 6500 2021, CAPACITY TJ 6500 2022, CAPACITY TJ 9000 2021, CAPACITY TJ 9000 2022

## Defect

Capacity of Texas (Capacity of Texas) is recalling certain 2021-2022 TJ 5000, 6500, and 9000 vehicles.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gears to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering gears, as necessary, free of charge.  The manufacturer has not yet provided a schedule for recall notification.  Owners may contact Capacity of Texas customer service at 1-800-323-0135.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
