| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Vehicles

SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2012, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2013, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2014, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2015, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2016, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2017, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2018, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2019, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2020, SCHWARZE A7 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2021, SCHWARZE A8 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2012, SCHWARZE A8 CNG STREET SWEEPER 2013 and others

## Defect

Alamo Group Inc. (Alamo) is recalling certain 2012-2021 Schwarze A7 CNG, A8 CNG, and A9 CNG street sweepers mounted on Freightliner M2-112 chassis and equipped with a horizontal exhaust system.  Lightweight debris, such as leaves, may build up in front of the sweeping head and accumulate against the chassis exhaust system.

## Consequence

Debris against the exhaust system increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install an exhaust tailpipe extension tube, free of charge.  Owners notification letters were mailed on January 14, 2022. Owners may contact Alamo Group customer service at 1-830-379-1480.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
