| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V574000 |
| Report received | 2025-09-04 |

| Component | POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:INTERNAL:PARK PAWL |
| Potential units affected | 75 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

DODGE CHARGER EV 2024, DODGE CHARGER EV 2025, JEEP WAGONEER S 2024, JEEP WAGONEER S 2025

## Defect

Chrysler (FCA US, LLC) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Dodge Charger and Jeep Wagoneer S electric vehicles. An incorrectly installed spring may cause the loss of the park function when the vehicleâs gear selector is placed in the park (P) position, which can result in a vehicle rollaway. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 114, "Theft Protection and Rollaway Prevention."

## Consequence

A vehicle rollaway increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the electric drive module, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 25, 2025. Owners may contact Chrysler customer service at 800-853-1403. Chrysler's number for this recall is 63C. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 11, 2025.
