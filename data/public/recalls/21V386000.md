# NHTSA recall 21V386000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V386000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-25 |
| Manufacturer | Seagrave Fire Apparatus, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 164 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2017, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2018, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2019, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2020, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2017, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2018, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2019, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2020, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2017, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2018, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2019, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2020

## Defect

Seagrave Fire Apparatus, LLC (Seagrave) is recalling certain 2017-2020 Marauder, Attacker and Capitol vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the rail threads, and replace the rail as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on June 18, 2021.  Owners may contact Seagrave customer service at 1-877-777-5360.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
