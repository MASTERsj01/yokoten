# NHTSA recall 25V523000: Fuel System, Other

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V523000 |
| Report received | 2025-08-12 |
| Manufacturer | The Heil Co. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER |
| Potential units affected | 222 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

HEIL FRONT END LOADER GARBAGE 2024

## Defect

The Heil Co. (Heil) is recalling certain 2024 front end loader garbage trucks. The fuel shutoff solenoid may fail, resulting in an engine stall while driving.

## Consequence

An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Heil will remove the CNG automatic shutoff solenoid, free of charge. All repairs will be performed free of charge. All affected trucks were repaired by August 12, 2024. Owners may contact Heil's customer service at 1-866-275-4345.
