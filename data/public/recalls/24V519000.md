# NHTSA recall 24V519000: Visibility:Windshield Wiper/Washer

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V519000 |
| Report received | 2024-07-08 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER |
| Potential units affected | 34 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2022, NOVA BUS LFS 2023

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova Bus) is recalling certain 2022-2023 LFS buses.  The windshield wiper arms may be incorrectly aligned, which can cause the wiper blades to collide.

## Consequence

Damaged wiper blades can reduce visibility in certain weather conditions, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Nova Bus will inspect and realign the wiper blades, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed September 10, 2024.  Owners may contact Nova Bus customer service at 1-800-350-6682.  Nova Bus's number for this recall is CR5615.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
