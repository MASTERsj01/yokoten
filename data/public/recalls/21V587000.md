| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Report received | 2021-07-29 |
| Manufacturer | Subaru of America, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP |
| Potential units affected | 175968 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SUBARU ASCENT 2019, SUBARU ASCENT 2020, SUBARU BRZ 2018, SUBARU BRZ 2019, SUBARU FORESTER 2018, SUBARU IMPREZA 2018, SUBARU IMPREZA 2019, SUBARU IMPREZA 2020, SUBARU LEGACY 2018, SUBARU LEGACY 2019, SUBARU LEGACY 2020, SUBARU OUTBACK 2018 and others

## Defect

Subaru of America, Inc. (Subaru) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Ascent, 2018 Forester, 2018-2020 Impreza, Legacy, Outback, 2018-2019 BRZ, WRX, and Toyota 86 vehicles.  The low-pressure fuel pump inside the fuel tank may fail.

## Consequence

Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the low-pressure fuel pump, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed September 24, 2021.  Subaru and Toyota owners may call customer service at 1-844-373-6614.  Subaru's number for this recall is WRG-21.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
