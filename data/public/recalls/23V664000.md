# NHTSA recall 23V664000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V664000 |
| Report received | 2023-09-29 |
| Manufacturer | Brinkley RV |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 143 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BRINKLEY RV MODEL G 2024

## Defect

Brinkley RV is recalling certain 2024 Brinkley RV Model G fifth wheel toy haulers.  The power control system was manufactured with undersized 10-gauge wire instead of 6-gauge wire.

## Consequence

An undersized wire may overheat, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the power control system, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 16, 2023.  Owners may contact Brinkley customer service at 1-574-501-4280.  Brinkley's number for this recall is PCI-2023-01.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
