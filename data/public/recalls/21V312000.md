# NHTSA recall 21V312000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V312000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-04 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 1053 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN 2018, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN 2019, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS 2020, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS 2021, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS 2022

## Defect

Blue Bird Body Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2018-2022 All American buses.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

The remedy for this recall is still under development.  Owner notification letters were mailed on July 30, 2021.  Owners may contact Blue Bird customer service at 1-478-822-2242.  Blue Bird's number for this recall is R21BH-SB.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
