# NHTSA recall 23V400000: Electrical System: Instrument Cluster/Panel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V400000 |
| Report received | 2023-06-02 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL |
| Potential units affected | 11308 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR LEGEND 2020, AUTOCAR LEGEND 2021, AUTOCAR LEGEND 2022, AUTOCAR LEGEND 2023, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2016, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2017, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2018, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2019, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2020, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2021, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2022, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2023

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling certain 2016-2023 ACX (Xpeditor) and 2020-2023 DC (Legend) vehicles.  The park brake warning light may incorrectly illuminate during an air loss event.  As such, these trucks fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 101, "Control and Displays."

## Consequence

A park brake warning light that illuminates incorrectly increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the pressure switch, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 31, 2023.  Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611.  Autocar's numbers for this recall is ACX-2307 and DC-2303.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
