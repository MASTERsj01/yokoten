# NHTSA recall 21V374000: Electrical System:Wiring:Switches/Knobs/Buttons

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V374000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-21 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:SWITCHES/KNOBS/BUTTONS |
| Potential units affected | 93 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER COLUMBUS 2021

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2021 Columbus Fifth Wheel trailers.  The breakaway switch and trailer brakes may not activate when needed due to an incorrectly wired breakaway switch.

## Consequence

Failure of the breakaway switch and trailer brakes can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will rewire the breakaway switch, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 9, 2021.  Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-821-1487.  Forest River's number for this recall is 410-1350.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
