# NHTSA recall 24V549000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V549000 |
| Report received | 2024-07-23 |
| Manufacturer | Temsa |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 7 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

TEMSA TS35 2024

## Defect

Temsa is recalling certain 2024 TS35 vehicles equipped with certain Cummins L9 diesel engines.  The remote fuel filter hoses may become separated from the fitting, which can result in a fuel leak and a lack of fuel supply to the engine.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.  Additionally, a lack of fuel can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Cummins will replace the fuel filter hoses, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed August 20, 2024.  Owners may contact Temsa customer service at 1-833-628-3672.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
