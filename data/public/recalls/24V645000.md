# NHTSA recall 24V645000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Exhaust System:Manifold/Header/Muffler/Tail Pipe

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V645000 |
| Report received | 2024-10-29 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM:MANIFOLD/HEADER/MUFFLER/TAIL PIPE |
| Potential units affected | 80 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR LEGEND 2022, AUTOCAR LEGEND 2023, AUTOCAR LEGEND 2024

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling certain 2022-2024 DC (Legend).  The horizontal exhaust diffuser may be improperly positioned and vent exhaust toward the CNG fuel tank.

## Consequence

Exhaust fumes can overheat the fuel tank, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and reposition the exhaust diffuser as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 25, 2024.  Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611.  Autocar's number for this recall is DC-2406.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
