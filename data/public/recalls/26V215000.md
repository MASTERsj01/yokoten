# NHTSA recall 26V215000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Cooling System:Radiator Cover/Winter Front

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V215000 |
| Report received | 2026-04-03 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM:RADIATOR COVER/WINTER FRONT |
| Potential units affected | 189 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2025, AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2026

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling certain 2025-2026 ACX (Xpeditor) vehicles. The winter front cover may interfere with the forward collision warning (FCW) system.

## Consequence

Interference may cause the FCW system to fail, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Autocar will replace the cover, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 29, 2026. Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611. Autocar's number for this recall is ACX-2602.
