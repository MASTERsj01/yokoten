# NHTSA recall 25V241000: Fuel System, Diesel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V241000 |
| Report received | 2025-04-11 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL |
| Potential units affected | 34 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2025, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2026, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2025, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2026, ENTEGRA redundant ASPIRE 2025

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2025 Entegra Aspire, 2025-2026 Entegra Anthem, and Cornerstone motorhomes.  The fuel filter housing may become damaged from contact with the rear cap support bracket, causing a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a new mounting plate and relocate the rear cap support bracket, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 9, 2025.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number for this recall is 9903625.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1- 888-275-9171), or go to www.safercar.gov.
