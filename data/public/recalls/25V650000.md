# NHTSA recall 25V650000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Exhaust System:Manifold/Header/Muffler/Tail Pipe

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V650000 |
| Report received | 2025-09-25 |
| Manufacturer | Altec Industries, Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM:MANIFOLD/HEADER/MUFFLER/TAIL PIPE |
| Potential units affected | 268 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ALTEC AA SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2021, ALTEC AA SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2022, ALTEC AA SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2023, ALTEC AA SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2024, ALTEC AA SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2025, ALTEC AM SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2024, ALTEC AM SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2025, ALTEC AN SERIES AERIAL DEVICE 2025

## Defect

Altec Industries, Inc. (Altec) is recalling certain 2021-2025 AA Series, 2024-2025 AM Series, and 2025 AN Series aerial devices. The outrigger pad holder may have been installed too close to the exhaust outlet.

## Consequence

The exhaust may overheat the outrigger pad, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Altec will instruct owners to inspect the outrigger pad holder placement.  A kit to relocate the outrigger pad holder or exhaust outlet will be provided as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 21, 2025. Owners may contact Altec customer service at 1-877-462-5832. Altec's number for this recall is CSN-3252.
