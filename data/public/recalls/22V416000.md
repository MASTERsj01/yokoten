# NHTSA recall 22V416000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V416000 |
| Report received | 2022-06-13 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS; ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM:FAN |
| Potential units affected | 273 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2019, NOVA BUS LFS 2020, NOVA BUS LFS 2021, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2019, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2020, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2021, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2022

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova Bus) is recalling certain 2019-2021 LFS and 2019-2022 LFS Artic buses.  The radiator fan's electrical fuse holder may experience excessive corrosion which can result in an electrical short.

## Consequence

An electrical short increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Nova Bus will provide the remedy parts and service document instructions, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on June 29, 2022.  Owners may contact Nova Bus' customer service at 1-800-350-6682.  Nova Bus' number for this recall is CR5259.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
