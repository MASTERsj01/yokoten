# NHTSA recall 23V904000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V904000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-29 |
| Manufacturer | Cake 0 Emission USA Inc |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 264 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CAKE KALK 2018, CAKE KALK 2019, CAKE KALK 2020, CAKE KALK 2021, CAKE KALK 2022, CAKE KALK 2023

## Defect

Cake 0 Emission USA Inc (Cake) is recalling certain 2018-2023 Kalk&, Kalk Ink&, and Kalk :work motorcycles.  The lithium-ion battery cells can fail while charging.

## Consequence

Battery cell failure increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Cake will replace the battery pack, free of charge.  As an interim action, owners will be instructed to check their battery serial number and if necessary, follow interim charging instructions until the remedy battery is available.  Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk were mailed January 19, 2024.  A second notice will be sent once the remedy becomes available, anticipated March 2024.  Owners may contact Cake customer service at service@ridecake.com.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
