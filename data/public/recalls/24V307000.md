# NHTSA recall 24V307000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V307000 |
| Report received | 2024-04-30 |
| Manufacturer | Braun Ambulances |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 29 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

BRAUN AMBULANCES CHIEF XL TYPE I 2023, BRAUN AMBULANCES CHIEF XL TYPE I 2024, BRAUN AMBULANCES CHIEF XL TYPE III 2023, BRAUN AMBULANCES CHIEF XL TYPE III 2026, BRAUN AMBULANCES EXPRESS PLUS TYPE I 2023, BRAUN AMBULANCES EXPRESS PLUS TYPE I 2024, BRAUN AMBULANCES EXPRESS TYPE III 2023, BRAUN AMBULANCES EXPRESS TYPE III 2024, BRAUN AMBULANCES LIBERTY TYPE I 2023, BRAUN AMBULANCES LIBERTY TYPE I 2024

## Defect

Braun Ambulances (Braun Ambulances) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Chief XL I, Chief XL III, Express Plus I, Express III, and Liberty I vehicles.  In the event of a short circuit or wiring failure, the firmware in the node may fail to shut off the output pin, resulting in overloaded electrical circuits.

## Consequence

Overloaded electrical circuits can overheat, increasing the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the firmware, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 17, 2024.  Owners may contact Braun Ambulances customer service at 1-877-344-9990.  Braun Ambulances number for this recall is 24-003.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
