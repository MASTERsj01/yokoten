# NHTSA recall 21V558000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V558000 |
| Report received | 2021-07-22 |
| Manufacturer | Suzuki Motor USA, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 270 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SUZUKI UH200 2019, SUZUKI UH200 2020

## Defect

Suzuki Motor USA, LLC (Suzuki) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Burgman 200 UH200 scooters.  The fuel hose assembly may leak fuel.

## Consequence

A fuel leak increases the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel hose assembly, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 20, 2021.  Owners may contact Suzuki customer service at (714) 572-1490.  Suzuki's number for this recall is 2A97.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
