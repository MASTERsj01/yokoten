# NHTSA recall 23V528000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V528000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-28 |
| Manufacturer | Midway Specialty Vehicles |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 4 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MIDWAY SPECIALTY VEHICLES FORD TRANSIT 2022

## Defect

Midway Specialty Vehicles (Midway) is recalling certain 2022 Ford Transit vans equipped with certain Cummins Onan generators.  The fuel hose between the inline fuel filter and the Injection Pump Module (IPM) may have inadequate clamp force, possibly resulting in a gasoline leak.

## Consequence

A gasoline leak in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel hose and clamps as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 26, 2023.  Owners may contact Midway customer service at 1-574-364-2530 ext. 103.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
