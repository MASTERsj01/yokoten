## Vehicles

EAST TO WEST ENTRADA 2025, EAST TO WEST ENTRADA 2026, FOREST RIVER FORESTER 2025, FOREST RIVER FORESTER 2026, FOREST RIVER FREELANDER 2025, FOREST RIVER FREELANDER 2026, FOREST RIVER LEPRECHAUN 2025, FOREST RIVER LEPRECHAUN 2026, FOREST RIVER SOLERA 2025, FOREST RIVER SOLERA 2026, FOREST RIVER SUNSEEKER 2025, FOREST RIVER SUNSEEKER 2026

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2025-2026 Sunseeker, Solera, Forester, Freelander, Leprechaun, and East to West Entrada motorhomes. The fuel line extension connectors  may have inconsistent crimps, allowing the fuel line to detach.

## Consequence

A detached fuel line can leak, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel lines as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed beginning September 26, 2025. Entrada owners may contact customer service at 1-574-264-6664. Forester, Solera, and Sunseeker owners may contact customer service at 1-574-206-7600. Leprechaun and Freelander owners may contact customer service at 1-574-825-8602. Forest River's number for this recall is 51-1955.
