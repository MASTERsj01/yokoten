# NHTSA recall 22V032000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V032000 |
| Report received | 2022-01-26 |
| Manufacturer | Pierce Manufacturing |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 243 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

PIERCE ARROW XT 2015, PIERCE ARROW XT 2016, PIERCE ARROW XT 2017, PIERCE ARROW XT 2018, PIERCE ARROW XT 2019, PIERCE ARROW XT 2020, PIERCE ARROW XT 2021, PIERCE ENFORCER 2015, PIERCE ENFORCER 2016, PIERCE ENFORCER 2017, PIERCE ENFORCER 2018, PIERCE ENFORCER 2019 and others

## Defect

Pierce Manufacturing (Pierce) is recalling certain 2015-2021 Arrow XT, Enforcer, Quantum and Velocity fire trucks.  The fuel tubes between the fuel rail and the injectors for cylinders four, five, and six may fatigue and crack, which can result in a high pressure fuel leak.

## Consequence

A high pressure fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

On engines with 75 miles or less, dealers will install vibration isolators to the fuel tubes.  Engines with more than 75 miles will receive new fuel tubes with vibration isolators, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on March 11, 2022.   Owners may contact Pierce's customer service at 1-888-974-3723.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
