| Field | Value |
|---|---|

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2019, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2020, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2021, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2022, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2023, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2024, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2025, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2026, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2027, BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2019, BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2020, BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS (ELECTRIC) 2021 and others

## Defect

Blue Bird Bus Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2019-2027 Vision and All American school buses. A loose 12-volt cable may disconnect, causing a loss of electrical systems.

## Consequence

A loss of brake assist, drive power, steering assist, or instrument panel displays can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect, add a torque stripe, and properly tighten the 12-volt cable as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 17, 2026. Owners may contact Blue Bird customer service at 1-478-822-2242.
