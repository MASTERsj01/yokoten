# NHTSA recall 24V052000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V052000 |
| Report received | 2024-01-30 |
| Manufacturer | Shyft Group |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 797 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SPARTAN K2 2023, SPARTAN K2 2024, SPARTAN K2 2025, SPARTAN K3 2023, SPARTAN K3 2024, SPARTAN K3 2025, SPARTAN K4 2023, SPARTAN K4 2024, SPARTAN K4 2025

## Defect

Shyft Group is recalling certain 2023-2025 Spartan RV K2, K3, and K4 chassis.  The steering gear may have foreign material inside the gear that could build pressure within the system, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of power steering assist can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Shyft Group will remove and replace the steering gear, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed March 21, 2024.  Owners may contact Shyft Group customer service at 1-800-237-7806.  Shyft Group's number for this recall is 24-01.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
