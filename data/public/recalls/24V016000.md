# NHTSA recall 24V016000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V016000 |
| Report received | 2024-01-12 |
| Manufacturer | E-One Incorporated |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 280 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

E-ONE CYCLONE E 2023, E-ONE CYCLONE II 2022, E-ONE CYCLONE II 2023, E-ONE CYCLONE N 2022, E-ONE CYCLONE N 2023, E-ONE TYPHOON N 2022, E-ONE TYPHOON N 2023

## Defect

E-One Incorporated (E-One) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Cyclone 2, Cyclone N, Typhoon N, and 2023 Cyclone E vehicles.  The steering assist bracket may have been machined incorrectly.

## Consequence

The incorrectly machined bracket may cause a reduction in hydraulic force, requiring greater steering effort and increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering assist bracket, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 7, 2024.  Owners may contact E-One customer service at 1-352-237-1122.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
