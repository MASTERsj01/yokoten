# NHTSA recall 21V864000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V864000 |
| Report received | 2021-11-05 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 479 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

WESTERN STAR 49X 2021, WESTERN STAR 49X 2022

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC. (DTNA) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Western Star 49X vehicles.  The steering shaft may not have been properly installed, causing it to separate from the steering gear.

## Consequence

Steering shaft separation can result in a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Owners are advised to not drive their vehicles until the inspection has been performed.  Dealers will inspect the steering shaft and repair it, as necessary, free of charge.   Owner notification letters were mailed January 12, 2022.  Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712.  DTNA's number for this recall is 905.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
