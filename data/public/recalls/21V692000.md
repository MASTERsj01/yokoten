# NHTSA recall 21V692000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V692000 |
| Report received | 2021-09-03 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 175 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

COACHMEN NOVA 2021, COACHMEN NOVA 2022

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Coachmen Nova motorhomes.  The Absorbed Glass Mat (AGM) battery was incorrectly installed inside the vehicle, which can allow hydrogen gas to enter the vehicle.

## Consequence

Hydrogen gas inside the vehicle can increase the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a lithium battery, free of charge.  Owners were notified of the defect on September 27, 2021.  Owner notification letters were mailed November 1, 2021. Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-825-6319 or 1-574-825-6625.  Forest River's number for this recall is 225-1416.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
