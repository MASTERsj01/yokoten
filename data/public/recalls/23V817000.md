# NHTSA recall 23V817000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V817000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-05 |
| Manufacturer | Textron Specialized Vehicles |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 67 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CUSHMAN HAULER PRO LSV 2024

## Defect

Textron Specialized Vehicles (Cushman) is recalling certain 2024 Cushman Hauler Pro LSV vehicles.  Water may enter the battery composite module and damage electrical circuits.

## Consequence

Damaged electrical circuits can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the recall repair is complete.  Dealers will inspect and replace the battery as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed January 22, 2024.  Owners may contact Cushman Customer Care at 1-888-438-3946.  Cushman 's number for this recall is FSB03-23.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
