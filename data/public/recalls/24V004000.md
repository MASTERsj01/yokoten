# NHTSA recall 24V004000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V004000 |
| Report received | 2024-01-05 |
| Manufacturer | Mickey Truck Bodies, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 37 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICKEY TRUCK BODIES CHEVROLET 4500 2021, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES CHEVROLET 5500 HD 2021, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES CHEVROLET 5500 HD 2022, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES CHEVROLET 5500 HD 2023, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES CHEVROLET LCF 2022, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES FREIGHTLINER M2 2020, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES FREIGHTLINER M2 2021, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES FREIGHTLINER M2 2022, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES HINO L6 2021, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES HINO L6 2022, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES HINO L6 2023, MICKEY TRUCK BODIES ISUZU FTR 2022 and others

## Defect

Mickey Truck Bodies, Inc. is recalling certain 2020-2022 Freightliner M2, 2020-2023 Isuzu NQR, 2021-2023 Chevrolet 5500 HD, Hino L6, 2021 Chevrolet 4500, Mack MD, 2022 Chevrolet LCF, and Isuzu FTR vehicles.  The 12-Volt wire harness on the truck refrigeration unit (TRU) may be exposed and missing heat shrink, which can cause electrical arcing.

## Consequence

Electrical arcing can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and secure/insulate exposed wires of the TRU, add a connector, and remove a fuse, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed January 16, 2024.  Owners may contact Mickey Truck Bodies customer service at 1-336-882-6806.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
