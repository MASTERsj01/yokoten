# NHTSA recall 22V872000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V872000 |
| Report received | 2022-11-23 |
| Manufacturer | Navistar, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 196 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

IC BUS CESB 2023

## Defect

Navistar, Inc. (Navistar) is recalling certain 2023 IC CE vehicles.  The fuel vapor line without the heat shield tubing may have been installed, exposing the line to heat from the exhaust system.

## Consequence

Heat exposure may cause the line to leak, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel vapor line with heat shield tubing, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed January 20, 2023.  Owners may contact Navistar's customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  Navistar's number for this recall is 22526.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
