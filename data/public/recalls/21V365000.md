# NHTSA recall 21V365000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V365000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-18 |
| Manufacturer | New Flyer of America, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 3600 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NEW FLYER XD35 2017, NEW FLYER XD35 2018, NEW FLYER XD35 2019, NEW FLYER XD35 2020, NEW FLYER XD35 2021, NEW FLYER XD40 2017, NEW FLYER XD40 2018, NEW FLYER XD40 2019, NEW FLYER XD40 2020, NEW FLYER XD40 2021, NEW FLYER XD60 2016, NEW FLYER XD60 2017 and others

## Defect

New Flyer of America, Inc. (New Flyer) is recalling certain 2017-2021 XD40, XD35, 2016-2021 XD60, 2017 XDE35, 2016-2020 XDE40, XDE60 vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

New Flyer will inspect the rail threads, and replace the rail as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on June 28, 2021.  Owners may contact New Flyer customer service at 1-204-224-6706.  New Flyer's number for this recall is R21-008.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
