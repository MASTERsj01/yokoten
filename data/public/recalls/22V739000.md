# NHTSA recall 22V739000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V739000 |
| Report received | 2022-10-05 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 2559 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS 2022, BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS 2023

## Defect

Blue Bird Body Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Vision school buses equipped with a 7.3L gasoline engine.  The transmission cable may not have sufficient clearance from the gasoline fuel supply line at the left rear corner of the engine, which can result in a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and reroute the transmission cable, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 9, 2023.  Owners may contact Blue Bird's customer service at 1-478-822-2242.  Blue Bird's number for this recall is R22CB (School Bus).

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
