# NHTSA recall 23V893000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V893000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-22 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Coaches North America |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 66 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MERCEDES-BENZ TOURRIDER BUSINESS 2022, MERCEDES-BENZ TOURRIDER BUSINESS 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ TOURRIDER BUSINESS 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ TOURRIDER PREMIUM 2022, MERCEDES-BENZ TOURRIDER PREMIUM 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ TOURRIDER PREMIUM 2024

## Defect

Daimler Coaches North America (Daimler Coaches) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Tourrider Business and Premium coaches.  The 120-Volt passenger outlets may not be secured properly, allowing them to detach.

## Consequence

A detached 120-Volt outlet can shock passengers, increasing the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a cavity socket and quadruple screw connection, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 13, 2024.  Owners may contact Daimler Coaches customer service at 1-416-624-3558.  Daimler Coaches number for this recall is 54.10T23277A.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
