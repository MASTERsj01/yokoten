# NHTSA recall 23V349000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V349000 |
| Report received | 2023-05-17 |
| Manufacturer | Foretravel, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 67 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FORETRAVEL FS450 2022, FORETRAVEL FS450 2023, FORETRAVEL FS450 2024, FORETRAVEL REALM 2022, FORETRAVEL REALM 2023, FORETRAVEL REALM 2026, FORETRAVEL REALM PRESIDENTIAL 2022, FORETRAVEL REALM PRESIDENTIAL 2023, FORETRAVEL REALM PRESIDENTIAL 2024

## Defect

Foretravel, Inc. (Foretravel) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Realm, Realm Presidential, and FS450 motorhomes.  The vehicle control module (ICM) may have a software error, which can cause a loss of drive power or disable other safety systems, such as the headlights or windshield wipers.

## Consequence

The loss of drive power, headlights, or windshield wipers can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will reprogram the vehicle control module, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 3, 2023.  Owners may contact Foretravel customer service at 1-800-955-6226.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
