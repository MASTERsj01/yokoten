# NHTSA recall 21V635000: Fuel System, Gasoline

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V635000 |
| Report received | 2021-08-12 |
| Manufacturer | Jaguar Land Rover North America, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE; FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP:MOUNTING PLATE/FLANGE |
| Potential units affected | 111746 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

LAND ROVER LR4 2010, LAND ROVER LR4 2011, LAND ROVER LR4 2012, LAND ROVER LR4 2013, LAND ROVER LR4 2014, LAND ROVER LR4 2015, LAND ROVER LR4 2016, LAND ROVER RANGE ROVER SPORT 2010, LAND ROVER RANGE ROVER SPORT 2011, LAND ROVER RANGE ROVER SPORT 2012, LAND ROVER RANGE ROVER SPORT 2013

## Defect

Jaguar Land Rover North America, LLC (Land Rover) is recalling certain 2010-2013 Range Rover Sport and 2010-2016 LR4 vehicles equipped with 3.0L V6 or 5.0L V8 engines.  The fuel outlet flange mounted on the fuel tank may crack, possibly resulting in a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel outlet flange, free of charge.  Interim owner notification letters were mailed October 7, 2021.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 11, 2022.  Owners may contact Land Rover customer service at 1-800-637-6837.  Land Rover's number for this recall is N623.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
