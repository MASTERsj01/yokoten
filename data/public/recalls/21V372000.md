# NHTSA recall 21V372000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V372000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-20 |
| Manufacturer | Newmar Corporation |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 1294 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2017, NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2018, NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2019, NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2020, NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2021, NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2022, NEWMAR MOUNTAIN AIRE 2017, NEWMAR MOUNTAIN AIRE 2018, NEWMAR MOUNTAIN AIRE 2019, NEWMAR NEW AIRE 2021, NEWMAR VENTANA 2019, NEWMAR VENTANA 2020 and others

## Defect

Newmar Corporation (Newmar) is recalling certain 2017-2022 Dutch Star, 2017-2019 Mountain Aire, 2019-2021 Ventana and 2021 New Aire motorhomes.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the rail threads, and replace the rail as necessary, free of charge.  Interim notification letters were mailed to owners on July 16, 2021.  Owners may contact Newmar customer service at 1-800-731-8000.  Newmar's number for this recall is Cummins 21E-032.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
