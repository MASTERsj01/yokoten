# NHTSA recall 26V594000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V594000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-11 |
| Manufacturer | Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 197 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICRO BIRD G5e SCHOOL BUS 2025, MICRO BIRD G5e SCHOOL BUS 2026

## Defect

Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. (Micro Bird) is recalling certain 2025-2026 G5e school buses. A wiring harness may have been incorrectly routed. A metal bracket may contact and damage the harness, causing a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A loss of driver power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Micro Bird will inspect, replace, and properly secure the wiring harness as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 10, 2026. Owners can contact Micro Bird's Customer Service at 819-477-2012. Micro Bird's number for this recall is 26-122-HUS.
