# NHTSA recall 22V429000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V429000 |
| Report received | 2022-06-16 |
| Manufacturer | Navistar, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 3570 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

IC BUS CESB 2022, IC BUS CESB 2023

## Defect

Navistar, Inc. (Navistar) is recalling certain 2022-2023 IC CE School Buses.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gear to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the steering gear serial numbers, and replace the steering gears as necessary, free of charge.  Interim owner notification letters were mailed August 15, 2022.  Remedy parts are expected to become available in mid-October 2022.  Owners may contact Navistar customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  Navistar's number for this recall is 22514.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
