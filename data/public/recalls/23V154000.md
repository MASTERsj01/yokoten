# NHTSA recall 23V154000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V154000 |
| Report received | 2023-03-08 |
| Manufacturer | Navistar, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 791 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

INTERNATIONAL HV 2023, INTERNATIONAL MV 2023, INTERNATIONAL MV 2024

## Defect

Navistar, Inc. (Navistar) is recalling certain 2023 International HV and 2023-2024 International MV vehicles.  The assembly bolts used to attach the battery box to the mounting bracket may be loose or missing.

## Consequence

Loose or missing bolts can allow the battery box to detach from the vehicle and become a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace and tighten all four bolts, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 28, 2023.  Owners may contact Navistar's customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  Navistar's number for this recall is 23506.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
