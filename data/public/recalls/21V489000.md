# NHTSA recall 21V489000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V489000 |
| Report received | 2021-06-29 |
| Manufacturer | East Manufacturing Corp. |
| Component | STEERING; STEERING:LINKAGES:KNUCKLE:SPINDLE:ARM |
| Potential units affected | 295 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

EAST MANUFACTURING MMX 2018, EAST MANUFACTURING MMX 2019, EAST MANUFACTURING MMX 2020, EAST MANUFACTURING MMX 2021

## Defect

East Manufacturing Corp. (East Manufacturing) is recalling certain 2018-2021 MMX trailers equipped with Ingersoll Smart-Steer self steer axles.  The steering arm may detach from the steering knuckle during certain low-speed, high-side loading maneuvers.

## Consequence

A detached steering arm may cause a loss of steering control.  In addition, attachment hardware may fall and become a road hazard.  Both of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the steering arm and apply welds, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on May 19, 2021.  Owners may contact East Manufacturing customer service at 1-352-861-3612.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
