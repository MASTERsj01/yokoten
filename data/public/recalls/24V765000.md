# NHTSA recall 24V765000: Visibility:Windshield Wiper/Washer

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V765000 |
| Report received | 2024-10-15 |
| Manufacturer | Lamborghini (Automobili Lamborghini) |
| Component | VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER |
| Potential units affected | 455 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

LAMBORGHINI REVUELTO 2024

## Defect

Lamborghini (Automobili Lamborghini) is recalling certain 2024 Revuelto vehicles.  The connecting rod between the passenger wiper arm and the wiper motor may bend and separate from the wiper motor, causing the wipers to fail.

## Consequence

Inoperative windshield wipers can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the passenger wiper system connecting rod, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed November 15, 2024, and November 22, 2024.  Owners may contact Automobili Lamborghini customer service at 1-866-681-6276.  Automobili Lamborghini's number for this recall is L74X-R.02.24.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
