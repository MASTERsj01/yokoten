# NHTSA recall 26V210000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V210000 |
| Report received | 2026-04-01 |
| Manufacturer | Isuzu Motors Limited |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 16759 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 4500HD 2025, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 4500HD 2026, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 4500HD 2027, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 4500XD 2025, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 4500XD 2026, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 4500XD 2027, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 5500XD 2025, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 5500XD 2026, ISUZU CHEVROLET LCF 5500XD 2027, ISUZU NPR HD 2025, ISUZU NPR HD 2026, ISUZU NPR HD 2027 and others

## Defect

Isuzu Motors Limited (Isuzu) is recalling certain 2025-2027 Chevrolet Low Cab Forward 5500XD, 4500XD, Isuzu NRR, NPR HD, NPR XD, 2025-2027 Chevrolet Low Cab Forward 4500HD, and Isuzu NRR EV vehicles. The chassis and/or cab wire harnesses may chafe against metal components, leading to harness failure.

## Consequence

Harness failure may cause an engine stall or the brake lights and parking brake to fail, increasing the risk of a crash. Damaged wires may also melt, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will add additional fasteners, inspect and replace the harnesses as necessary, free of charge. This is a Phased campaign. Phase 1 will include NRR EV vehicles. Owner notification letters were mailed April 24, 2026 to NRR EV owners. Phase 2 will include Isuzu N-Series and Chevrolet LCF Series vehicles. Owner notification letters were mailed May 22, 2026 to Isuzu N-Series owners. Phase 3 will include Chevrolet LCF Series owners. Owner notification letters were mailed July 10, 2026 to Chevrolet LCF Series owners. Owners will receive another notice once remedy becomes available. Owners may contact Isuzu customer service at 1-866-441-9638. Isuzu's number for this recall is V2602.
