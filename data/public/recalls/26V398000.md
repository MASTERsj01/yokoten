# NHTSA recall 26V398000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V398000 |
| Report received | 2026-06-22 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 1824 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER IBEX 2025, FOREST RIVER IBEX 2026, FOREST RIVER NO BOUNDARIES 2025, FOREST RIVER NO BOUNDARIES 2026, FOREST RIVER RPOD 2025, FOREST RIVER RPOD 2026, FOREST RIVER RPOD 2027

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2025-2026 NOBO, Ibex, and 2025-2027 RPOD recreational trailers. The 110V circuits on the distribution panel may be mislabeled, which can result in electrical shock.

## Consequence

Electrical shock increases the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the circuit labels, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 20, 2026. Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-825-0856. Forest River's number for this recall is 47-2072.
