# NHTSA recall 25V475000: Electrical System: Instrument Cluster/Panel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V475000 |
| Report received | 2025-07-21 |
| Manufacturer | Rosenbauer America |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL |
| Potential units affected | 788 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ROSENBAUER AVENGER 2019, ROSENBAUER AVENGER 2020, ROSENBAUER AVENGER 2021, ROSENBAUER AVENGER 2022, ROSENBAUER COMMANDER 2019, ROSENBAUER COMMANDER 2020, ROSENBAUER COMMANDER 2021, ROSENBAUER COMMANDER 2022, ROSENBAUER COMMANDER 2023, ROSENBAUER COMMANDER 2024, ROSENBAUER WARRIOR 2019, ROSENBAUER WARRIOR 2020 and others

## Defect

Rosenbauer America (Rosenbauer) is recalling certain 2019-2022 Avenger, 2019-2024 Commander, and Warrior fire trucks equipped with an Innovative Controls instrument panel. A software error may cause the odometer to freeze and not display accurate mileage.

## Consequence

An inaccurate odometer reading can unintentionally delay critical safety-related maintenance, potentially impairing the safe operation of the vehicle and increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the instrument panel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 10, 2025. Owners may contact Rosenbauer customer service at 1-651-462-1000.
