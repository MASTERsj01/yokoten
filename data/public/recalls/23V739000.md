# NHTSA recall 23V739000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V739000 |
| Report received | 2023-11-03 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 24 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER SANDSTORM 2023, FOREST RIVER SANDSTORM 2024, FOREST RIVER SHOCKWAVE 2024, FOREST RIVER STEALTH 2023, FOREST RIVER STEALTH 2024

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Sandstorm, Stealth, and 2024 Shockwave travel trailers.  The wire routed from the 50AMP inlet to the transfer switch in the distribution panel is the incorrect gauge, which may cause the wire to melt.

## Consequence

A melted wire may cause a short circuit, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the incorrect 10-gauge wire with a 6-gauge wire, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed November 20, 2023.  Owners may contact Forest River Customer Service at 1-951-357-2327.  Forest River's number for this recall is 67-1700.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
