# NHTSA recall 23V001000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V001000 |
| Report received | 2023-01-05 |
| Manufacturer | Volvo Car USA, LLC |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 74 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

VOLVO V60CC 2019, VOLVO V60CC 2020, VOLVO V60CC 2021, VOLVO V60CC 2022, VOLVO XC60 2019, VOLVO XC60 2020, VOLVO XC60 2021, VOLVO XC60 2022, VOLVO XC90 2019, VOLVO XC90 2020, VOLVO XC90 2021, VOLVO XC90 2022

## Defect

Volvo Car USA, LLC (Volvo Car) is recalling certain 2019-2022 V60CC, XC60, and XC90 vehicles.  During steering gear assembly, double screws may have been assembled on top of each other, which can cause a screw to fall into the gearbox housing.

## Consequence

A screw inside the gearbox housing can cause the steering wheel to lock up and increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the steering gear, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed March 1, 2023.  Owners may contact Volvo Car's customer service at 1-800-458-1552.  Volvo Car's number for this recall is R10213.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
