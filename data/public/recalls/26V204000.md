# NHTSA recall 26V204000: Visibility:Windshield Wiper/Washer:Linkages

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V204000 |
| Report received | 2026-03-31 |
| Manufacturer | Ford Motor Company |
| Component | VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:LINKAGES |
| Potential units affected | 422613 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FORD EXPEDITION 2021, FORD EXPEDITION 2022, FORD EXPEDITION 2023, FORD F-250 SD 2022, FORD F-250 SD 2023, FORD F-350 SD 2022, FORD F-350 SD 2023, FORD F-450 SD 2022, FORD F-450 SD 2023, FORD F-550 SD 2022, FORD F-550 SD 2023, FORD F-600 SD 2022 and others

## Defect

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Lincoln Navigator, Expedition, and 2022-2023 F-250 SD, F-350 SD, F-450 SD, F-550 SD, and F-600 SD vehicles. The windshield wiper arms may break, causing the wipers to fail.

## Consequence

Windshield wiper failure can reduce visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the wiper arms, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 5, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S24.Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on April 1, 2026.
