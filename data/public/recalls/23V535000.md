# NHTSA recall 23V535000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V535000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-31 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 54 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2023

## Defect

Autocar, LLC. (Autocar) is recalling certain 2023 ACX (Xpeditor) vehicles, equipped with Cummins L9 diesel engines.  The remote Stage 2 fuel filter hoses may have been damaged during production, allowing the hoses to leak or disconnect.

## Consequence

A disconnected hose will result in an engine stall increasing the risk of a crash.  Also, a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Cummins dealers will replace both hoses, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed September 28, 2023.  Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611.  Autocar's number for this recall is ACX-2308.  This recall is an expansion of recall number 23E-055.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
