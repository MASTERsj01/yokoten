# NHTSA recall 26V412000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V412000 |
| Report received | 2026-06-29 |
| Manufacturer | Tiffin Motorhomes, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 425 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TIFFIN ALLEGRO BAY 2026, TIFFIN ALLEGRO BAY 2027, TIFFIN ALLEGRO BREEZE 2026, TIFFIN ALLEGRO BUS 2026, TIFFIN ALLEGRO BUS 2027, TIFFIN ALLEGRO OPEN ROAD 2026, TIFFIN ALLEGRO OPEN ROAD 2027, TIFFIN ALLEGRO RED 2026, TIFFIN ALLEGRO RED 2027, TIFFIN BYWAY 2026, TIFFIN GT1 2026, TIFFIN GT1 2027 and others

## Defect

Tiffin Motorhomes, Inc. (Tiffin) is recalling certain 2026-2027 Zephyr, Wayfarer, Phaeton, Open Trail, GT1, Allegro Red, Allegro Open Road, Allegro Bus, Allegro Bay, 2026 Midas, Byway, and Allegro Breeze recreational vehicles, equipped with certain MaxxAir N-Series Maxxfan rooftop ventilation fans. The printed circuit board may fail during certain operations and overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated printed circuit board increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the circuit board, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 26, 2026. Owners may contact Tiffin customer service at 1-256-356-8661. Tiffin's number for this recall is TIF-149. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning August 28, 2026.
