# NHTSA recall 21V099000: Steering:Critical Fasteners

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V099000 |
| Report received | 2021-02-19 |
| Manufacturer | E-One Incorporated |
| Component | STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS |
| Potential units affected | 10 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

E-ONE CYCLONE II 2020, E-ONE TYPHOON 2020, E-ONE TYPHOON N 2020

## Defect

E-One Incorporated (E-One) is recalling certain 2020 Cyclone 2, Typhoon, and Typhoon N vehicles.  The steering gear mounting bolts may become loose and back out, resulting in difficulty steering.

## Consequence

Difficulty steering, or the inability to steer in both directions, can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

E-One will notify owners, and dealers will replace the steering gear mounting bolts and washers, free of charge.  The recall is expected to begin March 2021.  Owners may contact E-One customer service at 1-352-237-1122.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
