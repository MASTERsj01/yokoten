# NHTSA recall 22V401000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V401000 |
| Report received | 2022-06-07 |
| Manufacturer | Mack Trucks, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 5644 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MACK ANTHEM (AN) 2021, MACK ANTHEM (AN) 2022, MACK GRANITE (GR) 2021, MACK GRANITE (GR) 2022, MACK LR 2021, MACK LR 2022, MACK PINNACLE (PI/PN) 2021, MACK PINNACLE (PI/PN) 2022, MACK TE 2021, MACK TERRAPRO (TE) 2022

## Defect

Mack Trucks, Inc. (Mack) is recalling certain 2021-22 Granite, Anthem, LR, Pinnacle, and Terrapro tractor trailers. The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gear to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the steering gear, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on July 29, 2022. Owners may contact Mack customer service at 1-800-866-1177. Mack's number for this recall is SC0437.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
