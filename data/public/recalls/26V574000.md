# NHTSA recall 26V574000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V574000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-04 |
| Manufacturer | Kibbi, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 505 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

RENEGADE CLASSIC 2026

## Defect

Kibbi, LLC (Kibbi) is recalling certain 2026-2027 Renegade Valencia, Verona, Vera Cruz, Vienna, Villagio, Verona LE, XL, Garage, Totter, Explorer TS, Explorer, and Classic recreational vehicles equipped with MaxxAir N-Series Maxxfan rooftop ventilation fans. The printed circuit board may fail during certain operations and overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated printed circuit board increases the risk of a fire.
