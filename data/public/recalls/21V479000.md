# NHTSA recall 21V479000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V479000 |
| Report received | 2021-06-25 |
| Manufacturer | SVI Trucks |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 60 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SVI TRUCKS RESCUE TRUCK 2017, SVI TRUCKS RESCUE TRUCK 2018, SVI TRUCKS RESCUE TRUCK 2019, SVI TRUCKS RESCUE TRUCK 2020, SVI TRUCKS RESCUE TRUCK 2021

## Defect

SVI Trucks is recalling certain 2017-2021 Rescue Truck vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the rail threads and fuel lines, and replace the rail as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on June 30, 2021.  Owners may contact SVI Truck customer service at 1-888-784-1112.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
