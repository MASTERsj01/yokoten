# NHTSA recall 25V205000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V205000 |
| Report received | 2025-04-02 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 34 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2024, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2025, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2024, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2025, ENTEGRA REATTA XL 2025

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Entegra Anthem, Aspire, and 2025 Reatta XL motorhomes, equipped with Cummins L9 diesel engines.  The remote fuel filter hose may separate from the fitting, causing a fuel leak and an engine stall.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.  An engine stall increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Cummins will replace the fuel hoses, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 15, 2025.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 888-327-4236 (TTY 888-275-9171) or go to nhtsa.gov.
