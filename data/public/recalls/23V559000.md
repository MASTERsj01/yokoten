# NHTSA recall 23V559000: Power Train:Axle Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V559000 |
| Report received | 2023-08-07 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AXLE ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 40 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER SANDPIPER 2024, FOREST RIVER SIERRA 2024

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2024 Sandpiper, and Sierra fifth wheels.  The axle may be installed backwards, which can reduce braking ability.

## Consequence

Reduced braking ability can increase the distance required to stop the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will remove and reinstall the axle, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 28, 2023.  Owners may contact Forest River Customer Service at 1-574-534-4574.  Forest River's number for this recall is 3-1665.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
