# NHTSA recall 26V538000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V538000 |
| Report received | 2026-08-20 |
| Manufacturer | Rossmonster Vans LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 85 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

ROSSMONSTER BAJA 2026, ROSSMONSTER HAVN 2026, ROSSMONSTER SKYE 2026

## Defect

Rossmonster Vans LLC (Rossmonster) is recalling certain 2026 Skye, Baja, and Havn motorcoaches, equipped with certain MaxxAir N-Series Maxxfan rooftop ventilation fans. The printed circuit board may fail during certain operations and overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated printed circuit board increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the printed circuit board as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 18, 2026. Owners may contact Rossmonster customer service at 1-720-295-0623.
