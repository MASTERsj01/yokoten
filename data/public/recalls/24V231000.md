# NHTSA recall 24V231000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Cooling System:Hoses/Lines/Piping/Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V231000 |
| Report received | 2024-03-27 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM:HOSES/LINES/PIPING/FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 653 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2020, NOVA BUS LFS 2021, NOVA BUS LFS 2022, NOVA BUS LFS 2023, NOVA BUS LFS 2024

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova Bus) is recalling certain 2020-2024 LFS vehicles.  The engine coolant return hose may fail, leaking coolant and causing an engine shutdown.

## Consequence

An unexpected engine shutdown can increase the risk of a crash.  Additionally, transferring passengers from the disabled bus can increase the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Nova Bus will replace the engine coolant return hose, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 03, 2024.  Owners may contact Nova Bus customer service at 1-800-350-6682.  Nova Bus number for this recall is CR5577.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
