# NHTSA recall 25V567000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V567000 |
| Report received | 2025-08-29 |
| Manufacturer | Motor Coach Industries |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY; ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY:MANAGEMENT SYSTEM/ENERGY CONTROL MODULE (BMS/BECM):SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 59 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MCI D45CRTE 2024, MCI D45CRTELE 2021, MCI D45CRTELE 2023, MCI D45CRTELE 2024, MCI J4500E 2020, MCI J4500E 2021, MCI J4500E 2023, MCI J4500E 2025

## Defect

Motor Coach Industries (MCI) is recalling certain 2020-2021 J4500E, 2021 D45CRTELE, 2023 J4500E, 2023-2024 D45CRTELE, 2024 D45CRTE, and 2025 J4500E buses. An inadequate weld on the high voltage battery busbar and incorrect software may cause the battery module to overheat.

## Consequence

A battery module that overheats increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

MCI will inspect and replace the battery module as necessary. MCI will also update the BMS software. Owner notification letters were mailed October 6, 2025.  Owners may contact MCI customer service at 1-800-241-2947.
