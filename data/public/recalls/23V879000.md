# NHTSA recall 23V879000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Inverter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V879000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-21 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:INVERTER |
| Potential units affected | 26 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FCCC MT50E 2023, FCCC MT50E 2024, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 JOULEY 2024

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain 2024 Thomas Built SAF-T-LINER C2 Jouley and 2023-2024 FCCC MT50E electric transit buses.  The high voltage inverter cable may not be secured properly, which can result in a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and tighten the fasteners as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 1, 2024.  Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712.  DTNA's number for this recall is FL987.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
