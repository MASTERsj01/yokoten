# NHTSA recall 21V058000: Electrical System:Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V058000 |
| Report received | 2021-02-05 |
| Manufacturer | Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 1292258 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MERCEDES-BENZ A 220 2019, MERCEDES-BENZ A 220 2020, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG A35 2020, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2017, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2018, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2019, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2020, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2021, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 2017, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 2018, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 2019, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 2020 and others

## Defect

Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2016 - 2021 CLA-Class, GLA-Class, GLE-Class, GLS-Class, SLC-Class, A-Class, GT-Class, C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS-Class, SL-Class, B-Class, GLB-Class, GLC-Class, and G-Class vehicles.  Please refer to the Part 573 report for specific vehicle model details.  The software design of the communication module may fail to communicate the correct vehicle location for the emergency call system (eCall) in the event of a crash.

## Consequence

Failure of the eCall system may result in emergency responders being dispatched to the wrong location, increasing the risk of injury following a crash.

## Remedy

MBUSA will notify owners, and the communication module software will be updated by a dealer, or through an over-the-air (OTA) update, free of charge.  The recall began March 12, 2021.  Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267.  MBUSA's number for this recall is 2021020025.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
