# NHTSA recall 21V658000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V658000 |
| Report received | 2021-08-25 |
| Manufacturer | SUMMIT TRAILER SALES, INC. |
| Component | STEERING; STEERING:LINKAGES:KNUCKLE:SPINDLE:ARM |
| Potential units affected | 1 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SUMMIT AD45 2017

## Defect

Summit Trailer Sales, Inc. (Summit Trailer) is recalling one 2017 AD45 trailer equipped with Dexter Ingersoll Smart-Steer self-steer axles.  The steering arm may detach from the steering knuckle during certain low-speed, high-side loading maneuvers.

## Consequence

A detached steering arm may cause a loss of steering control.  In addition, attachment hardware may fall and become a road hazard.  Both of these scenarios can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

A dealer will inspect the steering arm and apply welds, free of charge.  Owners may contact Summit Trailer's customer service at 1-570-754-3511.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
