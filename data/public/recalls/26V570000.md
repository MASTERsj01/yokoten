# NHTSA recall 26V570000: Electrical System:Propulsion System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V570000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-02 |
| Manufacturer | Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 8521 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TOYOTA C-HR 2026

## Defect

Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2026 C-HR battery electric vehicles. A software programming error may cause the electric drive system battery to become overcharged under certain driving conditions, which can shut down the electric drive system and result in a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

An unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the battery electric vehicle electronic control unit (BEV ECU) software, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 18, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's number for this recall is 26TA17.
