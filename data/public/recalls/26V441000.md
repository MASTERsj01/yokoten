# NHTSA recall 26V441000: Electrical System:Starter Assembly:Relay

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V441000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-08 |
| Manufacturer | BMW of North America, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:STARTER ASSEMBLY:RELAY |
| Potential units affected | 29119 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BMW 330E IPERFORMANCE 2016, BMW 330E IPERFORMANCE 2017, BMW 330E IPERFORMANCE 2018, BMW 530E IPERFORMANCE 2018, BMW 530E IPERFORMANCE 2019, BMW 530E IPERFORMANCE 2020, BMW 530E XDRIVE IPERFORMANCE 2018, BMW 530E XDRIVE IPERFORMANCE 2019, BMW 530E XDRIVE IPERFORMANCE 2020, BMW 740LE XDRIVE IPERFORMANCE 2017, BMW 740LE XDRIVE IPERFORMANCE 2018, BMW 740LE XDRIVE IPERFORMANCE 2019

## Defect

BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2018-2020 530e xDrive, 2017-2019 740Le xDrive, 2018-2020 530e iPerformance, 2016-2018 330e iPerformance vehicles. The engine starter relay may corrode, causing the relay to overheat and short circuit.

## Consequence

A short circuit in the starter relay may increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Owners are advised to park outside and away from structures until the remedy is complete. Dealers will replace the engine starter, free of charge. Letters informing owners of the safety risk were mailed September 3, 2026.  Another letter will be mailed after the remedy is confirmed. Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.  Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning August 28, 2026.
