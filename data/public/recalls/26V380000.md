# NHTSA recall 26V380000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V380000 |
| Report received | 2026-06-09 |
| Manufacturer | Alliance RV, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 1198 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN ATHLETIC SERIES 2023, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN ATHLETIC SERIES 2024, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN BUSINESS CLASS 2024, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN BUSINESS CLASS 2025, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN BUSINESS CLASS 2026, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN DAYCRUISER 2023, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN DAYCRUISER 2024, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN DAYCRUISER 2025, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN DAYCRUISER 2026, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN G-45 2023, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN G-45 2024, MIDWEST AUTOMOTIVE DESIGN G-45 2025 and others

## Defect

Alliance RV, LLC (Alliance) is recalling certain 2020-2026 Midwest Automotive Design Luxe Cruiser, 2021-2026 Ultimate Coach, G-55, 2022 Ultimate Entertainer, 2022-2024 Revolution, 2022-2025 Professional Series, 2022-2026 Ultimate Cruiser, Ultimate Limo, Patriot Cruiser, Ultimate Traveler, 2023 Signature, 2023-2024 Athletic Series, 2023-2026 Day Cruiser, Freedom, G-45, 2024-2026 Business Class, and Ultimate Weekender recreational vehicles. The ground wire may be undersized for the electrical system.

## Consequence

The undersized ground cable may overheat, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the configuration and replace the inverter ground cable as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 24, 2026. Owners may contact Alliance customer service at 1-574-218-7165. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning June 22, 2026.
