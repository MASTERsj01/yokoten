# NHTSA recall 24V213000: Fuel System, Diesel: Pre-Heater

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V213000 |
| Report received | 2024-03-20 |
| Manufacturer | Prevost Car (US) Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL: PRE-HEATER |
| Potential units affected | 37 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

PREVOST H3-45 2024, PREVOST H3-45 VIP 2010, PREVOST X3-45 2024

## Defect

Prevost Car (US) Inc. (Prevost) is recalling certain 2010 H3-45 VIP, 2024 H3-45 and X3-45 vehicles.  The coolant heaters may generate excessive exhaust gas and overheat.

## Consequence

Excessive exhaust gas may increase the risk of carbon monoxide poisoning, and an overheating heater increases the risk of a fire.  Additionally, a vehicle fire may require an evacuation procedure in an uncontrolled traffic situation, increasing the risk of injury to passengers.

## Remedy

Vehicle owners are advised not to operate the heaters until they have been repaired.  Prevost will inspect the coolant heaters, replace the damaged components, and clean the heater's internal components, as necessary.  In addition, Prevost will adjust the air to fuel ratio.  All repairs will be performed free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 17, 2024.  Owners may contact Prevost customer service at 1-866-870-2046.  Prevost's number for this recall is SR24-13.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
