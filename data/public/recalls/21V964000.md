# NHTSA recall 21V964000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V964000 |
| Report received | 2021-12-13 |
| Manufacturer | Seagrave Fire Apparatus, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 174 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2019, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2020, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2021, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2019, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2020, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2021, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2019, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2020, SEAGRAVE MARAUDER 2021

## Defect

Seagrave Fire Apparatus, LLC (Seagrave) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Marauder, Attacker, and Capitol vehicles equipped with Cummins X12 engines.  The fuel tubes between the fuel rail and the injectors for cylinders four, five, and six may fatigue and crack, which can result in a high pressure fuel leak.

## Consequence

A high pressure fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

On engines with 75 miles or less, dealers will install vibration isolators to the fuel tubes.  Engines with more than 75 miles will receive new fuel tubes with vibration isolators.  Repairs will be performed free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 11, 2022. Owners may contact Cummins customer service at 1-800-286-6467.  Seagrave's number for this recall is C2532.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
