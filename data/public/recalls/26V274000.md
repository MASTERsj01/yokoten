# NHTSA recall 26V274000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Cooling System:Hoses/Lines/Piping/Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V274000 |
| Report received | 2026-04-29 |
| Manufacturer | Storyteller Overland |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM:HOSES/LINES/PIPING/FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 357 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

STORYTELLER OVERLAND BEAST MODE 2023, STORYTELLER OVERLAND CLASSIC 2023, STORYTELLER OVERLAND STEALTH 2023

## Defect

Storyteller Overland, LLC (Storyteller) is recalling certain 2023 Stealth, Classic, and Beast Mode vehicles equipped with AWD. Improperly routed coolant lines may overheat the engine, causing a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will remove the secondary heat exchanger loop, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed June 24, 2026. Owners may contact Storyteller customer service at 1-888-999-7442.
