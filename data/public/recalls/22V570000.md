# NHTSA recall 22V570000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V570000 |
| Report received | 2022-08-04 |
| Manufacturer | Ferrara Fire Apparatus, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 108 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FERRARA CINDER 2021, FERRARA CINDER 2022, FERRARA IGNITER 2021, FERRARA IGNITER 2022, FERRARA INFERNO 2021, FERRARA INFERNO 2022

## Defect

Ferrara Fire Apparatus, Inc. (Ferrara) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Inferno, Igniter, and Cinder fire trucks.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gears to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering gears, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on September 1, 2022.  Owners may contact Ferrara's customer service at 1-800-443-9006.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
