# NHTSA recall 23V390000: Electrical System: Instrument Cluster/Panel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V390000 |
| Report received | 2023-06-01 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL |
| Potential units affected | 62 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

JAYCO MELBOURNE 2020, JAYCO MELBOURNE 2021, JAYCO MELBOURNE PRESTIGE 2020, JAYCO MELBOURNE PRESTIGE 2021

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2020-2021 Melbourne and Melbourne Prestige motorhomes.  The instrument cluster may fail to display any information when the vehicle is started.

## Consequence

A blank instrument panel will not show critical information, such as vehicle speed, fuel gauge, warning messages, and warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the instrument cluster software on the affected vehicles, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 15, 2023.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number for this recall is Daimler 23V-204.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
