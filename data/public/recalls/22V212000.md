# NHTSA recall 22V212000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V212000 |
| Report received | 2022-03-31 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 196 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

JAYCO TRAVEL TRAILER WHITE HAWK 2022

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2022 White hawk travel trailers, equipped with a 50-amp shore power inlet.  The power inlet lug bolts may have been manufactured with excessively hard metal, preventing them from being tightened properly and resulting in a loose wiring connection.

## Consequence

Loose wiring can cause an electrical arc or short, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the power inlet, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 20, 2022.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number for this recall is 9901575.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
