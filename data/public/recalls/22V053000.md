# NHTSA recall 22V053000: Steering:Linkages:Tie Rod Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V053000 |
| Report received | 2022-02-02 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 28 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ACCOLADE 2021, ENTEGRA ACCOLADE 2022, ENTEGRA ACCOLADE XL 2021, ENTEGRA ACCOLADE XL 2022, JAYCO SENECA 2021, JAYCO SENECA 2022, JAYCO SENECA PRESTIGE 2021, JAYCO SENECA PRESTIGE 2022

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Entegra Coach Accolade, Accolade XL, Jayco Seneca, and Seneca Prestige Class C motorhomes.  The tie rod clamps may be loose, which could result in loose tie rod ends that could break or detach.

## Consequence

Detached or broken tie rod ends can result in steering loss, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Daimler Truck dealers will replace the tie rod clamp bolts and nuts, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed March 15, 2022.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number for this recall is 21V-369.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
