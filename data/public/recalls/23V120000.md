# NHTSA recall 23V120000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Engine:Engine Control Module (Ecu/Ecm):Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V120000 |
| Report received | 2023-02-23 |
| Manufacturer | Navistar, Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM):SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 27972 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

IC BUS TC 2022, IC BUS TC 2023, INTERNATIONAL HV 2022, INTERNATIONAL HV 2023, INTERNATIONAL HV 2024, INTERNATIONAL MV 2022, INTERNATIONAL MV 2023, INTERNATIONAL MV 2024

## Defect

Navistar, Inc. (Navistar) is recalling certain 2022-2024 International HV, International MV trucks, and 2022-2023 IC Bus TC commercial buses.  When the parking brake is applied, the automatic transmission is still in drive or reverse position, and the stationary power take-off (PTO) switch is engaged by the operator, the engine may rev and overcome the parking brake's ability to keep the vehicle from moving.

## Consequence

Unexpected vehicle movement can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the Cummins ECM software, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed April 24, 2023.  Owners may contact Navistar's customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  Navistar's number for this recall is 23504.  This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 20V-810.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
