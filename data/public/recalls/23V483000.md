# NHTSA recall 23V483000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V483000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-13 |
| Manufacturer | Industrial Video & Control Co |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 4 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MVT-900A MVT-3001-G 2021

## Defect

Industrial Video & Control Co (IV & C) is recalling certain 2021 MVT-900A MVT-3001-G surveillance trailers, equipped with certain Cummins Onan QG 2800i and Onan QG 2800i EVAP generator sets.  The fuel hose between the inline fuel filter and the Injection Pump Module (IPM) may have inadequate clamp force, possibly resulting in a gasoline leak.

## Consequence

A gasoline leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel hose and clamps as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 8, 2023.  Owners may contact IV & C customer service at 1-617-467-3059.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
