# NHTSA recall 22V428000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V428000 |
| Report received | 2022-06-16 |
| Manufacturer | Navistar, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 33655 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

IC BUS TC 2022, IC BUS TC 2023, INTERNATIONAL HV 2022, INTERNATIONAL HV 2023, INTERNATIONAL HX 2022, INTERNATIONAL HX 2023, INTERNATIONAL LONESTAR 2022, INTERNATIONAL LONESTAR 2023, INTERNATIONAL LT 2022, INTERNATIONAL LT 2023, INTERNATIONAL MV 2022, INTERNATIONAL MV 2023 and others

## Defect

Navistar, Inc. (Navistar) is recalling certain 2022-2023 International HX, HV, LoneStar, LT, MV, RH, IC TC Commercial Bus, and  2022 International WorkStar vehicles.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gear to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the steering gear serial numbers, and replace the steering gears as necessary, free of charge.  Interim owner notification letters were mailed August 18, 2022.  Remedy parts are expected to become available in mid-October 2022.  Owners may contact Navistar customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  Navistar's number for this recall is 22513.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
