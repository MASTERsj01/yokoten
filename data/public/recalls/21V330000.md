# NHTSA recall 21V330000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V330000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-10 |
| Manufacturer | Eldorado National- California, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 266 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ELDORADO AXESS 2017, ELDORADO AXESS 2018, ELDORADO AXESS 2019, ELDORADO AXESS 2020, ELDORADO EZRM 2017, ELDORADO EZRM 2018, ELDORADO EZRM 2019, ELDORADO EZRM 2020, ELDORADO XHF 2017, ELDORADO XHF 2018, ELDORADO XHF 2019, ELDORADO XHF 2020

## Defect

Eldorado National-California, Inc. (Eldorado National-California) is recalling certain 2017-2020 Axess, EXRM, and XHF vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the rail as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on June 18, 2021.  Owners may contact Eldorado National-California customer service at 1-951-684-6868.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
