# NHTSA recall 22V602000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V602000 |
| Report received | 2022-08-08 |
| Manufacturer | Crane Carrier Company |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 391 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CRANE CARRIER LET2 2021, CRANE CARRIER LNT 2021, CRANE CARRIER LNT 2022, CRANE LET2 2022

## Defect

Crane Carrier Company (Crane Carrier) is recalling certain 2021-2022 LET2 and LNT vehicles.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gears to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering gears, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on September 1, 2022.  Owners may contact Crane Carrier's customer service at 330-308-6700.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
