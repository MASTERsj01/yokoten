# NHTSA recall 21V013000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V013000 |
| Report received | 2021-01-15 |
| Manufacturer | KZRV, L.P. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 806 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

KZRV ESCAPE 2021, KZRV SONIC 2021, KZRV SPORTSMEN CLASSIC 2021

## Defect

KZRV, L.P. (KZRV) is recalling certain 2021 Sportsmen Classic, Escape, and Sonic recreational trailers.  The microwave circuits were improperly connected to a 20amp circuit breaker instead of a 15amp circuit breaker.

## Consequence

Being connected to the wrong amperage circuit breaker may not provide adequate circuit protection, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

KZRV will notify owners, and dealers will replace the 20 amp circuit breaker with a 15 amp circuit breaker, free of charge.  The recall began February 22, 2021.  Owners may contact KZRV customer service at 1-800-768-4016, extension 154 or 153.  KZRV's number for this recall is KZ-2021-01.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
