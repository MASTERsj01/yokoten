# NHTSA recall 23V224000: Electrical System:Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V224000 |
| Report received | 2023-03-30 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 972 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2022, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2023, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2024, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2022, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2023, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2024, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2022, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2023, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2024, ENTEGRA REATTA 2022, ENTEGRA REATTA 2023, ENTEGRA REATTA 2024 and others

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Jayco Embark, Entegra Anthem, Aspire, Cornerstone, Reatta, and Reatta XL motorhomes.   A vehicle control module software error may cause loss of drive power or loss of key safety functions, such as loss of head lights or windshield wipers.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of drive power, or reduced visibility from a loss of head lights or windshield wipers can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will reprogram the vehicle control module, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed May 1, 2023.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
