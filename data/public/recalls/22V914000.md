# NHTSA recall 22V914000: Power Train:Automatic Transmission:Gear Position Indication (Prndl)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V914000 |
| Report received | 2022-12-09 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:GEAR POSITION INDICATION (PRNDL) |
| Potential units affected | 23 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FCCC MT50E 2021, FCCC MT50E 2022

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain 2021-2022 MT50e electric walk-in vans that were previously repaired under recall number 22V-340.  The transmission gear shifter indicator may not display accurately.  As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 102, "Transmission Shift Lever Sequence/Starter Interlock/Transmission Braking Effect."

## Consequence

Driving a vehicle with an inaccurate transmission gear shifter indicator can result in an unintentional gear shift, which can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the gear shifter, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 2, 2023.  Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712.  DTNA's number for this recall is FL-961.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
