## Vehicles

MOTIV E-450 BEV CHASSIS 2021, MOTIV E-450 BEV CHASSIS 2022, MOTIV E-450 BEV CHASSIS 2023, MOTIV E-450 BEV CHASSIS 2024, MOTIV F-53 BEV CHASSIS 2019, MOTIV F-53 BEV CHASSIS 2020, MOTIV F-59 BEV CHASSIS 2019, MOTIV F-59 BEV CHASSIS 2020, MOTIV F-59 BEV CHASSIS 2021, MOTIV F-59 BEV CHASSIS 2022, MOTIV F-59 BEV CHASSIS 2023

## Defect

Motiv Power Systems, Inc. (Motiv) is recalling certain 2021-2024 E-450, 2019-2023 F-59, and 2019-2020 F-53 vehicles equipped with a Motiv Gen 5 EPIC powertrain.  The vehicle control software could misclassify a loss of isolation between the chassis and high voltage system, shutting down the high voltage (HV) batteries.

## Consequence

The shutdown of the HV batteries can cause a loss of power steering, brake power assist, and drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Motiv will release an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed September 5, 2024.  Owners may contact Motiv customer service at 1-650-458-4804.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
