# NHTSA recall 25V276000: Visibility:Windshield Wiper/Washer:Linkages

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V276000 |
| Report received | 2025-04-24 |
| Manufacturer | International Motors, LLC |
| Component | VISIBILITY:WINDSHIELD WIPER/WASHER:LINKAGES |
| Potential units affected | 14786 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

INTERNATIONAL EMV 2025, INTERNATIONAL HV 2025, INTERNATIONAL HV 2026, INTERNATIONAL HX 2025, INTERNATIONAL HX 2026, INTERNATIONAL LT 2025, INTERNATIONAL LT 2026, INTERNATIONAL MV 2025, INTERNATIONAL MV 2026, INTERNATIONAL RH 2025, INTERNATIONAL RH 2026

## Defect

International Motors, LLC (International) is recalling certain 2025-2026 HV, HX, LT, MV, RH, and 2025 EMV vehicles.  The linkage that connects the right-side wiper arm to the motor may separate, causing the wipers to fail.

## Consequence

Windshield wiper failure can reduce driver visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the wiper module, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 2, 2025.  Owners may contact International customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  International's number for this recall is 25505.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 888-327-4236 (TTY 888-275-9171) or go to nhtsa.gov.
