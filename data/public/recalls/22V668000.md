# NHTSA recall 22V668000: Electrical System:Wiring:Switches/Knobs/Buttons

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V668000 |
| Report received | 2022-09-06 |
| Manufacturer | Keystone RV Company |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:SWITCHES/KNOBS/BUTTONS |
| Potential units affected | 536 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

DUTCHMEN ASPEN TRAIL 2023, DUTCHMEN COLEMAN 2023, DUTCHMEN KODIAK 2023

## Defect

Keystone RV Company (Keystone) is recalling certain 2023 Dutchmen Aspen Trail, Coleman, and Kodiak travel trailers.  The switch for the electric stabilizer jacks may not be waterproof from the back side of the switch, which can result in an electrical short.

## Consequence

An electrical short can cause the stabilizer jacks to extend during transit, increasing the risk of crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the jack switch, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed September 27, 2022.  Owners may contact Keystone customer service at 1-866-425-4369.  Keystone's number for this recall is 22-435.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
