# NHTSA recall 24V522000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V522000 |
| Report received | 2024-07-09 |
| Manufacturer | Grand Design RV, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 734 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

GRAND DESIGN REFLECTION 2024

## Defect

Grand Design RV, LLC (Grand Design) is recalling certain 2024 Reflection travel trailers.  The electrical wiring may be missing over current circuit protection.

## Consequence

Wiring without circuit protection may become damaged and short circuit, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a 50-amp breaker, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 24, 2024.  Owners may contact Grand Design customer service at 1-574-825-9679.  Grand Design's number for this recall is 910044.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
