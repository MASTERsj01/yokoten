# NHTSA recall 25V553000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Cooling System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V553000 |
| Report received | 2025-08-26 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 24 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2013

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova Bus) is recalling certain 2013 LFS transit buses. The coolant hoses routed through the HVAC roof unit may fail and leak coolant into the passenger seating area.

## Consequence

Hot coolant that leaks into the passenger seating area increases the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Nova Bus will inspect and replace the hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 22, 2025. Owners may contact Nova Bus customer service at 1-450-472-6410. Nova Bus' number for this recall is CR5752. Vehicles in this recall were previously recalled under 18V117.
