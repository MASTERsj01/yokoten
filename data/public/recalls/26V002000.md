# NHTSA recall 26V002000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V002000 |
| Report received | 2026-01-05 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 16 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN TRANSIT BUS (ELECTRIC) 2022, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN TRANSIT BUS (ELECTRIC) 2023, BLUE BIRD ALL AMERICAN TRANSIT BUS (ELECTRIC) 2024

## Defect

Blue Bird Bus Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2022-2024 All American transit buses. A loose 12-volt cable may disconnect, causing a loss of electrical systems.

## Consequence

A loss of brake assist, drive power, steering assist, or instrument panel displays can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect, add a torque stripe, and properly tighten the 12-volt cable as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 17, 2026. Owners may contact Blue Bird customer service at 1-478-822-2242.
