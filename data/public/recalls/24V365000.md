# NHTSA recall 24V365000: Fuel System, Other:Storage:Tank Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V365000 |
| Report received | 2024-05-23 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 112 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS 2021, BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS 2022, BLUE BIRD VISION SCHOOL BUS 2023

## Defect

Blue Bird Body Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2021-2023 Vision school buses.  The diesel tank that supplies the fuel fired heater was improperly designed and may leak fuel.  As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard number 301, "Fuel System Integrity."

## Consequence

A fuel leak onto the road creates a hazard for other road users, increasing the risk of a crash.  Additionally, a fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will repair the tank, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 08, 2024.  Owners may contact Blue Bird customer service at 1-478-822-2242.  Blue Bird's number for this recall is R24DA.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
