# NHTSA recall 21V586000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Fuel Pump

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V586000 |
| Report received | 2021-07-29 |
| Manufacturer | BMW of North America, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP |
| Potential units affected | 11216 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BMW 328D 2014, BMW 328D 2015, BMW 328D 2016, BMW 328D 2017, BMW 328D 2018, BMW 535D 2014, BMW 535D 2015, BMW 535D 2016, BMW 740LD 2015, BMW X3 2015, BMW X3 2016, BMW X3 2017 and others

## Defect

BMW of North America, LLC (BMW) is recalling certain 2014-2018 328d, 328d xDrive, X5 xDrive35d, 2014-2016 535d, 535d xDrive, 2015 740Ld xDrive, and 2015-2017 X3 xDrive28d vehicles.  The high-pressure fuel pump may fail.

## Consequence

Fuel pump failure can cause an engine stall while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the high-pressure fuel pump, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on January 14, 2022.  Owners may contact BMW customer service at 1-800-525-7417.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
