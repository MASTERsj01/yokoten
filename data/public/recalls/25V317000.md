# NHTSA recall 25V317000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V317000 |
| Report received | 2025-05-12 |
| Manufacturer | Aluminum Trailer Company (ATC) |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 12 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ATC PLA550 2025

## Defect

Aluminum Trailer Company (ATC) is recalling certain 2025 ATC PLA550 Bumper Pull vehicles. The power wire from the battery to the stabilizer jacks may be wired incorrectly without over current circuit protection.

## Consequence

Wiring without circuit protection may short circuit, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and repair the wiring and install a 30 AMP fuse, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 9, 2025.  Owners may call ATC customer service at 1-877-441-2440 ext. 342.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
