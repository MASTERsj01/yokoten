# NHTSA recall 26V087000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Cooling System:Hoses/Lines/Piping/Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V087000 |
| Report received | 2026-02-13 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:COOLING SYSTEM:HOSES/LINES/PIPING/FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 52 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER EFX 2023, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER EFX 2024, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER EFX 2025, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX 2023, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX 2024, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX 2025, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX 2026

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain Thomas Built Buses 2023-2025 Saf-T-Liner EFX and 2023-2026 Saf-T-Liner HDX vehicles. The heater hose clamps may not have been tightened properly, causing the hose to disconnect and leak heating coolant inside the passenger compartment.

## Consequence

Contact with hot heating coolant may burn occupants, increasing the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the heater hose clamps, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2026. Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712. DTNA's number for this recall is F1030.
