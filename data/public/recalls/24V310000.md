# NHTSA recall 24V310000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V310000 |
| Report received | 2024-05-01 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 6 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2023, ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2024, JAYCO TERRAIN 2023, JAYCO TERRAIN 2024

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Jayco Terrain, and Entegra Launch motorhomes. The main wiring harness may chafe against the drive shaft and short circuit, which can result in a loss of drive power, power steering, and brake system control.

## Consequence

A short circuit increases the risk of a fire.  In addition, a loss of drive power, power steering, and brake system control increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Mercedes dealers will inspect the routing of the main wiring harness and correct it, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 14, 2024.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
