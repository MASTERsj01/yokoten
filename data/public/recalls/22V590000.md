# NHTSA recall 22V590000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V590000 |
| Report received | 2022-08-05 |
| Manufacturer | Pierce Manufacturing |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 514 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

PIERCE ARROW XT 2021, PIERCE ARROW XT 2022, PIERCE DASH CF 2021, PIERCE DASH CF 2022, PIERCE ENFORCER 2021, PIERCE ENFORCER 2022, PIERCE IMPEL 2021, PIERCE IMPEL 2022, PIERCE QUANTUM 2021, PIERCE QUANTUM 2022, PIERCE VELOCITY 2021, PIERCE VELOCITY 2022

## Defect

Pierce Manufacturing (Pierce) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Velocity, Impel, Enforcer, Arrow XT, Quantum, and Dash CF trucks.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gears to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering gears, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on October 4, 2022.  Owners may contact Pierce's customer service at 1-888-974-3723.  Pierce's number for this recall is 74B321.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
