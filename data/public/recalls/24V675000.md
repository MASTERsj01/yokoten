# NHTSA recall 24V675000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V675000 |
| Report received | 2024-09-12 |
| Manufacturer | Alliance RV, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 154 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ALLIANCE RV PARADIGM 2024, ALLIANCE RV PARADIGM 2025

## Defect

Alliance RV, LLC (Alliance) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Paradigm 295MK, 310RL, 340RL, 370FB, 375RD, 380MP, 382RK, 385FL, and 395DS fifth wheels.  The 7-way electrical connection to the brake actuator may have been mis-wired, which can prevent the brakes from engaging if the tow vehicle detaches.

## Consequence

Brakes that fail to engage when a tow vehicle detaches increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and correct the wiring, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 29, 2024.  Owners may contact Alliance customer service at 1-574-218-7165.
