# NHTSA recall 22V769000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Storage:Tank Assembly:Filler Pipe And Cap

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V769000 |
| Report received | 2022-10-13 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY:FILLER PIPE AND CAP |
| Potential units affected | 42 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

JAYCO FIFTH WHEEL SEISMIC 2023

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2023 Seismic and Seismic Luxury trailers.  Fuel cap does not fit properly on the filler fuel neck for the cargo fuel tank.

## Consequence

An improperly fitted fuel cap may leak fuel or vapors, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace filler fuel neck as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 25, 2022.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number to this recall is 9901581.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
