# NHTSA recall 26V468000: Electrical System:Wiring:Front Underhood

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V468000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-20 |
| Manufacturer | Ford Motor Company |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FRONT UNDERHOOD |
| Potential units affected | 565691 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

FORD BRONCO 2021, FORD BRONCO 2022, FORD BRONCO 2023, FORD BRONCO 2024, FORD BRONCO 2025, FORD BRONCO 2026

## Defect

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021-2026 Bronco and Bronco Raptor vehicles. The engine compartment wiring harness may become damaged and short circuit.

## Consequence

A short circuit in the engine compartment can create heat or spark, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install sheathing over the wiring, free of charge. This is a Phased campaign. Phase 1 will include 2026 Bronco Raptor notified owners on 7/31/2026. Phase 2 will include 2021-2025 Bronco Raptor vehicle will notify owners on or about 8/14/2026. Phase 3 will include 2021-2026 Bronco will notify owners on or about 9/1/2026. Owner notification letters were mailed August 5, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S55. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall became searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 23, 2026.
