# NHTSA recall 24V181000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V181000 |
| Report received | 2024-03-07 |
| Manufacturer | Spartan Fire, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 42 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

KME redundant PANTHER 2024, SPARTAN FIRE COMMERCIAL 2024, SPARTAN FIRE FC-94 2024, SPARTAN FIRE GLADIATOR 2023, SPARTAN FIRE GLADIATOR 2024, SPARTAN FIRE METROSTAR 2023, SPARTAN FIRE METROSTAR 2024

## Defect

Spartan Fire, LLC (Spartan Fire) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Gladiator, MetroStar, 2024 Commercial, FC-94, and KME Panther trucks equipped with Hercules HC 1.7 multiplex nodes.  In the event of a short circuit or wiring failure, the firmware in the node may fail to shut off the output pin, resulting in overloaded electrical circuits.

## Consequence

Overloaded electrical circuits can overheat, increasing the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Weldon will update the firmware, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed April 8, 2024.  Owners may contact Spartan Fire customer service at 1-517-543-6400.  Spartan Fire's number for this recall is 24003.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
