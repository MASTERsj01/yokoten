# NHTSA recall 22V241000: Electrical System:Wiring:Relays/Solenoids

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V241000 |
| Report received | 2022-04-13 |
| Manufacturer | New England Wheels |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:RELAYS/SOLENOIDS |
| Potential units affected | 101 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NEW ENGLAND WHEELS FRONTRUNNER 2021, NEW ENGLAND WHEELS FRONTRUNNER 2022

## Defect

New England Wheels (New England) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Frontrunner buses.  The Programmable Relay Power Center (PRC) modules may fail.

## Consequence

Failure of the Programmable Relay Power Center (PRC) increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a fused wire jumper harness, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on April 20, 2022.  Owners may contact New England's customer service at 1-800-886-9247.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
