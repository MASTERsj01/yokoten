# NHTSA recall 23V582000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V582000 |
| Report received | 2023-08-15 |
| Manufacturer | Indian Motorcycle Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 45 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

INDIAN CHALLENGER 2023, INDIAN PURSUIT DARK HORSE 2023, INDIAN PURSUIT LIMITED 2023

## Defect

Indian Motorcycle Company (Indian) is recalling certain 2023 Challenger, Pursuit Dark Horse Icon w/Premium Package, Pursuit Dark Horse w/Premium Package, and Pursuit Limited w/Premium Package motorcycles.  The fuel line may have been improperly assembled, which can result in an engine stall.

## Consequence

An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel line, free of charge.  All motorcycles have been remedied.  Owners may contact Indian customer service at 1-877-204-3697.  Indian's number for this recall is I-23-06.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
