# NHTSA recall 21V326000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V326000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-07 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 9662 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FCCC XB CHASSIS 2018, FCCC XB CHASSIS 2019, FCCC XB CHASSIS 2020, FCCC XC CHASSIS 2018, FCCC XC CHASSIS 2019, FCCC XC CHASSIS 2020, FCCC XC CHASSIS 2021, FREIGHTLINER 108SD 2018, FREIGHTLINER 108SD 2019, FREIGHTLINER 108SD 2020, FREIGHTLINER 108SD 2021, FREIGHTLINER 114SD 2018 and others

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Freightliner Custom Chass XC Chassis, 108SD, Custom Chass XB Chassis, Business Class M2, 114SD, and Western Star 4700 vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore holes, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel rail and fuel lines, free of charge.  Interim owner notification letters were mailed on June 21, 2021.  Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712.  DTNA's number for this recall is FL-886.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
