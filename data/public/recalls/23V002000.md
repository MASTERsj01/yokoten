# NHTSA recall 23V002000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V002000 |
| Report received | 2023-01-06 |
| Manufacturer | Triple E Recreational Vehicles |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 341 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TRIPLE E UNITY 2021, TRIPLE E UNITY 2022, TRIPLE E UNITY 2023

## Defect

Triple E Recreational Vehicles (Triple E) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Unity U24IB and 2021-2023 Unity U24TB motorhomes.  The battery disconnect switch terminal posts may contact the metal grounded fridge frame, causing a short and blowing a 100 AMP fuse.

## Consequence

An electrical short-circuit can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install two rubber terminal post protective covers, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed January 17, 2023.  Owners may contact Triple E customer service at 1-877-992-9906.  Triple E's number for this recall is CA#10253-1.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
