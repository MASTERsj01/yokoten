# NHTSA recall 26V444000: Steering:Linkages:Knuckle:Spindle:Arm

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V444000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-09 |
| Manufacturer | Volvo Trucks North America |
| Component | STEERING:LINKAGES:KNUCKLE:SPINDLE:ARM |
| Potential units affected | 3858 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

VOLVO VAH 2026, VOLVO VHD 2026, VOLVO VN 2026, VOLVO VNL (4) 2026, VOLVO VNL (4) 2027, VOLVO VNR (4) 2026, VOLVO VNR (4) 2027

## Defect

Volvo Trucks North America (Volvo Trucks) is recalling certain 2026 VOLVO VAH, VN, VHD, 2026-2027 VNL (4) and VNR (4) vehicles. The steering knuckles may have been manufactured with insufficiently tightened steering arms.

## Consequence

A loose steering arm can result in a loss of vehicle steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and tighten the fasteners between the steering arm and steering knuckle, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 27, 2026. Owners may contact Volvo Trucks' customer service at 800-528-6586. Volvo Trucks' number for this recall is RVXX2607. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will become searchable on NHTSA.gov on July 16, 2026.
