# NHTSA recall 21V241000: Steering:Hydraulic Power Assist:Hose, Piping, And Connections

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V241000 |
| Report received | 2021-04-06 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Vans USA, LLC |
| Component | STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:HOSE, PIPING, AND CONNECTIONS |
| Potential units affected | 4444 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER (VS30) 2019, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 1500 2019, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 1500 2020, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 2500 2019, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 2500 2020, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 3500 2019, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 3500 2020, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 4500 2019, FREIGHTLINER SPRINTER 4500 2020, MERCEDES-BENZ SPRINTER (VS30) 2019, MERCEDES-BENZ SPRINTER 1500 2019, MERCEDES-BENZ SPRINTER 1500 2020 and others

## Defect

Daimler Vans USA, LLC (DVUSA) is recalling certain 2019-2020 Mercedes Benz Sprinter and Freightliner Sprinter vehicles.  The hydraulic power steering return line hoses may detach and cause hydraulic fluid to leak.

## Consequence

A hydraulic fluid leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

DVUSA will notify owners, and dealers will install a screw-type clamp, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on May 28, 2021.  Owners may contact DVUSA customer service at 1-877-762-8267.  DVUSA's number for this recall is NV3LETULPX.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
