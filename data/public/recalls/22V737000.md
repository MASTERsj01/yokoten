# NHTSA recall 22V737000: Electrical System:Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V737000 |
| Report received | 2022-10-04 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 962 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

COACHMEN BEYOND 2022, COACHMEN BEYOND 2023, COACHMEN GALLERIA 2022, COACHMEN GALLERIA 2023, COACHMEN NOVA 2022, COACHMEN NOVA 2023

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Coachmen Beyond, Galleria, and Nova recreational vehicles, equipped with certain Multiplex Electronic Control Modules (ECM).  The ECM software may cause the awning to unintentionally extend or retract when the vehicle is parked.

## Consequence

An awning that unintentionally extends or retracts increases the risk of injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the Electronic Control Module (ECM), free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 24, 2022.  Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-825-4995; Firefly customer service at 1-574-825-4600; or Coachmen customer service at 1-574-825-6319 or 574-825-6225.  Forest River's number for this recall is 225-1538.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
