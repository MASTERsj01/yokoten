# NHTSA recall 24V934000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V934000 |
| Report received | 2024-12-17 |
| Manufacturer | Cummins, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 205 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CUMMINS POWER GENERATION GENERATOR SET 2022, CUMMINS POWER GENERATION GENERATOR SET 2023, CUMMINS POWER GENERATION GENERATOR SET 2024

## Defect

Cummins, Inc. (Cummins) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Power Generation C275D2RE trailer-mounted generators. The unsecured wire leads may chafe against the starter motor housing or terminal and short circuit.

## Consequence

A short increases the risk of electrical shock or a fire.

## Remedy

Distributors or warranty repair locations will inspect and repair the generator set wiring harness and secure the wiring leads, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed February 7, 2025.  Owners may contact Cummins customer service at 1-800-286-6467.  Cummins' number for this recall is C5807.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
