# NHTSA recall 21V694000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V694000 |
| Report received | 2021-09-07 |
| Manufacturer | Eldorado National- California, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 3 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ELDORADO EZRM 2019, ELDORADO EZRM 2020, ELDORADO EZRM 2021

## Defect

Eldorado National-California, Inc. (Eldorado National-California) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Eldorado National EZRM vehicles equipped with L9 Diesel Engines with a remote Stage 2 fuel filter and pressurized hoses.  A fuel leak may occur from the fuel hose between the fuel pump and the remote fuel filter head.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Cummins will replace the fuel hoses, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed on September 20, 2021.  Owners may contact Eldorado National-California's customer service at 1-951-684-6868.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
