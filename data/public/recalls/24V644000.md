# NHTSA recall 24V644000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V644000 |
| Report received | 2024-08-29 |
| Manufacturer | Altec Industries, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 623 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ALTEC AT SERIES 2024, ALTEC SPRAY TRUCK 2024, ALTEC TA CLASS 2024

## Defect

Altec Industries, Inc. (Altec) is recalling certain 2024 AT and TA series aerial devices, and ST series spray bodies.  The fuel or brake lines may not be properly secured, which can cause the lines to become damaged and leak.

## Consequence

A brake fluid leak can cause brake failure, increasing the risk of a crash.  A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Altec will provide owners with instructions to inspect the brake and fuel lines.  If improperly secured, Altec will provide a kit to secure the lines.  If the lines are damaged, dealers will repair the fuel and brake lines.  Repairs will be performed free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on January 23, 2025.  Owners may contact Altec customer service at 1-877-462-5832.  Altec's number for this recall is CSN-3181.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
