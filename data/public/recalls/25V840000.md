# NHTSA recall 25V840000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V840000 |
| Report received | 2025-12-05 |
| Manufacturer | Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 2428 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2021, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2022, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2023, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2024, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2025, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2026, MICRO BIRD MB II SCHOOL BUS 2022, MICRO BIRD MB II SCHOOL BUS 2023, MICRO BIRD MB II SCHOOL BUS 2024, MICRO BIRD MB II SCHOOL BUS 2025, MICRO BIRD T-SERIES SCHOOL BUS 2022, MICRO BIRD T-SERIES SCHOOL BUS 2023 and others

## Defect

Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. (Micro Bird) is recalling certain 2022-2025 MB II, T-Series, and 2021-2026 G5 school buses. The power cable providing electricity to the air conditioning control board may have a loose connection to the power stud on the board.

## Consequence

A loose power cable connection may cause the control board to overheat and melt, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will secure the power cable and power stud as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 2, 2026. Owners can contact Micro Bird Customer Service at 819-477-2012. Micro Bird's number for this recall is 25-118-SUC.
