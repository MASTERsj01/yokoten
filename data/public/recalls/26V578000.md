# NHTSA recall 26V578000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Storage:Tank Assembly:Mounting

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V578000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-09 |
| Manufacturer | Ford Motor Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY:MOUNTING |
| Potential units affected | 223472 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FORD F-150 2023, FORD F-150 2024, FORD F-150 2025, FORD F-150 2026, FORD F-150 2027

## Defect

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2023-2027 F-150 vehicles. The fuel tank may be improperly secured and leak fuel, or detach from the vehicle while driving.

## Consequence

A detached tank can create a road hazard, increasing the risk of a crash. In addition, a fuel leak can cause an engine stall or increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel tank straps, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 21, 2026. Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332. Ford's number for this recall is 26S69. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning September 14, 2026.
