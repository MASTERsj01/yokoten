# NHTSA recall 23V755000: Power Train:Automatic Transmission:Park/Neutral Start Interlock Switch

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V755000 |
| Report received | 2023-11-09 |
| Manufacturer | Subaru of America, Inc. |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:PARK/NEUTRAL START INTERLOCK SWITCH |
| Potential units affected | 95830 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SUBARU CROSSTREK 2021, SUBARU FORESTER 2022, SUBARU LEGACY 2021, SUBARU LEGACY 2022, SUBARU LEGACY 2023, SUBARU OUTBACK 2021, SUBARU OUTBACK 2022, SUBARU OUTBACK 2023

## Defect

Subaru of America, Inc. is recalling certain model year 2021 Crosstrek, 2022 Forester, 2021-2023 Legacy, and Outback vehicles.  An insufficient weld may allow water to enter the inhibitor switch, causing it to fail.

## Consequence

An inoperative inhibitor switch may prevent the reverse lights from illuminating and the rearview camera image from displaying, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the inhibitor switch, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed January 8, 2024.  Owners may contact Subaru customer service at 1-800-782-2783.  Subaru's number for this recall is WRQ-23.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
