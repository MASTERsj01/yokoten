# NHTSA recall 22V714000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V714000 |
| Report received | 2022-09-23 |
| Manufacturer | Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 45 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2013, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2014, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2015, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2016, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2017, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2018, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2019, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2020, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2021, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2022, MICRO BIRD MB II TRANSIT BUS 2012, MICRO BIRD MB II TRANSIT BUS 2013 and others

## Defect

Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. (Micro Bird) is recalling certain 2013-2022 G5, 2015-2019 T-Series, and 2012-2015 MBII transit buses.  The power cable that provides electricity to the air conditioning control board may have a loose connection to the power stud on the board.

## Consequence

A loose power cable connection may cause the control board to overheat and melt, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will redirect the power cable and secure the power cable connect as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on March 2, 2023.  Owners may contact Micro Bird's customer service at 1-819-477-2012.  Micro Bird's number for this recall is 22-096-VUC.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
