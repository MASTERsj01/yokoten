# NHTSA recall 23V530000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V530000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-28 |
| Manufacturer | Chinook Motor Coach, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 31 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CHINOOK BAYSIDE 2020, CHINOOK BAYSIDE 2021, CHINOOK BAYSIDE 2022, CHINOOK BAYSIDE 2023, CHINOOK BAYSIDE 2024

## Defect

Chinook Motor Coach, LLC (Chinook) is recalling certain 2020-2024 Bayside motorhomes, equipped with certain Cummins Onan QG 2800i, and Onan QG 2800i EVAP generator sets.  The fuel hose between the inline fuel filter and the Injection Pump Module (IPM) may have inadequate clamp force, possibly resulting in a gasoline leak.

## Consequence

A gas leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Cummins dealers will inspect and replace the fuel hose and clamps, as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 11, 2023.  Owners may contact Chinook customer service at 1-574-584-3756.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
