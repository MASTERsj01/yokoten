# NHTSA recall 22V108000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V108000 |
| Report received | 2022-02-24 |
| Manufacturer | Tenco Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 1 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TOR DR-106100 2020

## Defect

Tenco Inc. (Tenco) is recalling one TOR DR-106100 highway carrier equipped with a Cummins X12 diesel engine.  The fuel tubes between the fuel rail and the injectors for cylinders four, five, and six may fatigue and crack, which can result in a high pressure fuel leak.

## Consequence

A high pressure fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

On engines with 75 miles or less, dealers will install vibration isolators to the fuel tubes.  Engines with more than 75 miles will receive new fuel tubes with vibration isolators, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on April 14, 2022.  Owners may contact Tenco's customer service at 1-450-388-1328.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
