# NHTSA recall 24V279000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V279000 |
| Report received | 2024-04-19 |
| Manufacturer | REV Recreation Group |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 79 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FLEETWOOD DISCOVERY 2022, FLEETWOOD DISCOVERY 2023, FLEETWOOD DISCOVERY 2024, HOLIDAY RAMBLER ENDEAVOR 2022, HOLIDAY RAMBLER ENDEAVOR 2023, HOLIDAY RAMBLER ENDEAVOR 2024

## Defect

REV Recreation Group (REV) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Fleetwood Discovery and Holiday Rambler Endeavor motorhomes, equipped with Remco 50-54 Rebel 4.0 GPM water pumps.  The water pump harness was designed with inadequate fuse protection.

## Consequence

Inadequate fuse protection may cause harness damage, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and relocate the water pump circuit to a properly sized fuse as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 15, 2024.  Owners may contact REV customer service at 1-800-509-3417.  REV's number for this recall is 240416REV.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
