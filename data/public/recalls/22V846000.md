# NHTSA recall 22V846000: Electrical System:Adas:Driver Monitoring:Camera/Sensor

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V846000 |
| Report received | 2022-11-16 |
| Manufacturer | Shyft Group |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ADAS:DRIVER MONITORING:CAMERA/SENSOR |
| Potential units affected | 543 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

UTILIMASTER AEROMASTER 2022

## Defect

Shyft Group (Shyft Group) is recalling certain 2022 Utilimaster Aeromaster vehicles.  The side-view image may display the wrong side of the vehicle when the turn signal and brake are applied at the same time.

## Consequence

An incorrect side-view image may reduce visibility and increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Shyft Group will install a jumper harness, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed December 2, 2022.  Owners may contact Shyft Group customer service at 1-800-582-3454.  Shyft Group's number for this recall is 22-18.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
