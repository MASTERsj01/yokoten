## Vehicles

MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2018, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2019, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2020, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2021, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2022, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2023, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2024, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2018, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2019, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2020, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2021, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2022 and others

## Defect

Alamo Group Inc. (Morbark) is recalling certain Morbark 2018-2024 EB1821, EB2131, EB2230, and M20 trailers.  The electrical wires in the Power Distribution Module (PDM) may be loose and contact the PDM plate, possibly resulting in electrical arcing between the wiring and plate.

## Consequence

Electrical arcing can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will secure the electrical wires, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 18, 2024.  Owners may contact Morbark's customer service at 1-800-255-8839.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
