# NHTSA recall 23V322000: Power Train:Driveline:Driveshaft

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V322000 |
| Report received | 2023-05-04 |
| Manufacturer | Navistar, Inc. |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:DRIVESHAFT |
| Potential units affected | 49 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

INTERNATIONAL MV 2023

## Defect

Navistar, Inc. (Navistar) is recalling certain 2023 International MV trucks.  The driveshaft tube seam weld may separate or tear, resulting in drive line separation and a sudden loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the driveshafts, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed July 3, 2023.  Owners may contact Navistar's customer service at 1-800-448-7825.  Navistar's number for this recall is 23513.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
