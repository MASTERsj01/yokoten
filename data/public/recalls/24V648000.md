# NHTSA recall 24V648000: Electrical System: Instrument Cluster/Panel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V648000 |
| Report received | 2024-09-03 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL |
| Potential units affected | 179 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2023, NOVA BUS LFS 2024, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2023

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova) is recalling certain 2023 LFS Artic and 2023-2024 LFS vehicles.  The instrument panel may fail to display the speedometer or incorrectly display warning lights, due to defective firmware.  In addition, the firmware may result in a loss of drive power.Â Â As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard numbers 101, "Control and Displays."

## Consequence

An instrument panel that fails to show critical safety information, such as the speedometer or warning lights, can increase the risk of a crash.  A loss of drive power also increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Novas Bus will update the instrument panel firmware, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2024.  Owners may contact Nova customer service at 1-800-350-6682.  Nova's number for this recall is CR5636.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
