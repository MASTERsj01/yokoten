# NHTSA recall 25V445000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Critical Fasteners

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V445000 |
| Report received | 2025-06-27 |
| Manufacturer | Orange EV |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:CRITICAL FASTENERS |
| Potential units affected | 20 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ORANGE EV HUSK-E 2023, ORANGE EV HUSK-E 2024, ORANGE EV HUSK-E 2025

## Defect

Orange EV (OEV) is recalling certain 2023-2025 HUSK-e terminal trucks previously remedied under Recall 25V367 (2025-SRC-04). The wrong size washer was installed in the thermal fuse bolted joint during the previous repair.

## Consequence

The incorrect washer can cause the bus bar connection to loosen or open, resulting in electrical arcing and increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

OEV will install the correctly sized washer, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 4, 2025. Owners may contact OEV customer service at 1-866-688-5223. OEV's number for this recall is 2025-SRC-05.
