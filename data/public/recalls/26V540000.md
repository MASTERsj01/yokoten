# NHTSA recall 26V540000: Electrical System:Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V540000 |
| Report received | 2026-08-20 |
| Manufacturer | Lucid USA, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE; ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 27185 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

LUCID AIR 2022, LUCID AIR 2023, LUCID AIR 2024, LUCID AIR 2025, LUCID AIR 2026

## Defect

Lucid USA, Inc. (Lucid) is recalling certain 2022-2026 Air vehicles. The exterior lighting circuit may have insufficient overcurrent protection, allowing the circuit to overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated circuit increases the risk of a fire. A loss of the center brake light or front lights can also decrease visibility, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Owners are advised to park their vehicle outside and away from structures until the recall remedy is completed. Lucid has released an over-the-air (OTA) software update, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 16, 2026. Owners may contact Lucid at 1-888-995-8243. Lucid's number for this recall is SR-26-06-0.
