# NHTSA recall 23V259000: Steering:Linkages:Tie Rod Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V259000 |
| Report received | 2023-04-07 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 48 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

THOMAS BUILT BUSES HDX 2023, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2023, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2024, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 JOULEY 2023, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 JOULEY 2024, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER EFX 2023, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER MVP-EF 2023

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain 2023 Thomas Built Buses SAF-T-LINER SAF-T-LINER C2 Jouley, Thomas Built Buses SAF-T-LINER EFX, Thomas Built Buses SAF-T-Liner HDX, Thomas Built Buses Chassis and 2023-2024 School Bus Chassis school buses.  The tie rod may be missing a fastener, allowing the steer arm to detach.

## Consequence

A detached steering arm can result in a sudden loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

DTNA will inspect and repair or replace the tie rod assembly as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 11, 2023.  Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712.  DTNA's number for this recall is FL970.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
