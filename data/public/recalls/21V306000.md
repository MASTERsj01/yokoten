# NHTSA recall 21V306000: Fuel System, Diesel:Fuel Injection System:Fuel Rail

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V306000 |
| Report received | 2021-04-30 |
| Manufacturer | Tiffin Motorhomes, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:FUEL INJECTION SYSTEM:FUEL RAIL |
| Potential units affected | 3198 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TIFFIN BUS 2017, TIFFIN BUS 2018, TIFFIN BUS 2019, TIFFIN BUS 2020, TIFFIN BUS 2021, TIFFIN PHAETON 2017, TIFFIN PHAETON 2018, TIFFIN PHAETON 2019, TIFFIN PHAETON 2020, TIFFIN PHAETON 2021

## Defect

Tiffin Motorhomes, Inc. (Tiffin) is recalling certain 2017-2021 Bus and Phaeton vehicles.  The sealing washer may not seat correctly in the pilot bore, allowing the high pressure fuel rail assembly to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Tiffin will work with Cummins to inspect the rail threads , and replace the rail and fuel lines if damage is found, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 11, 2021.  Owners may contact Tiffin customer service at 1-256-356-8661.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
