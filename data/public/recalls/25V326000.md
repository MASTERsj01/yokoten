# NHTSA recall 25V326000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V326000 |
| Report received | 2025-06-23 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 22271 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

WESTERN STAR 47X 2021, WESTERN STAR 47X 2022, WESTERN STAR 47X 2023, WESTERN STAR 47X 2024, WESTERN STAR 47X 2025, WESTERN STAR 47X 2026, WESTERN STAR 49X 2020, WESTERN STAR 49X 2021, WESTERN STAR 49X 2022, WESTERN STAR 49X 2023, WESTERN STAR 49X 2024, WESTERN STAR 49X 2025 and others

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America (DTNA) is recalling certain 2020-2026 Western Star 49X and 2021-2026 Western Star 47X vehicles. A stud connecting the battery to the starter at the frame rail may have been incorrectly installed, which can cause a short circuit.

## Consequence

A short circuit increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

DTNA will inspect and replace damaged cables and loose hardware. In addition, DTNA will properly tighten hardware and install dielectric tape. All repairs will be performed, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 1, 2025. Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712. DTNA's number for this recall is F1020. This recall is replaced by NHTSA recall number 26V079. Vehicles already repaired under this recall will need to have the new remedy completed.
