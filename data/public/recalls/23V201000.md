# NHTSA recall 23V201000: Electrical System: Integrated Trailer Brake Control

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V201000 |
| Report received | 2023-03-24 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INTEGRATED TRAILER BRAKE CONTROL |
| Potential units affected | 12 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER PAUSE 2023

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2023 Forest River Pause Travel trailers.  The breakaway switch may be incorrectly wired and cause the battery to not charge, resulting in no power to the breakaway switch.

## Consequence

A breakaway switch that does not have power will not activate the trailer brakes, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will rewire the breakaway switch, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 3, 2023.  Owners may contact Forest River Customer Service at 1-574-327-2681.  Forest River's number for this recall is 426-1613.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
