# NHTSA recall 21V767000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V767000 |
| Report received | 2021-09-28 |
| Manufacturer | Entegra Coach |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 119 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2021, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2022, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2021, ENTEGRA ASPIRE 2022, ENTEGRA REATTA XL 2021, ENTEGRA REATTA XL 2022

## Defect

Entegra Coach (Entegra) is recalling certain 2021-2022 Anthem, Aspire, and Reatta XL Class A vehicles equipped with Cummins L9 diesel engines that have remote Stage 2 fuel filters and pressurized hoses.  A fuel leak may occur in the fuel hoses between the fuel pump and the remote fuel filter head.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Entegra will work with Cummins to replace the fuel hoses, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on September 3, 2021.  Owners may contact Entegra customer service at 1-800-283-8267.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
