# NHTSA recall 21V363000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V363000 |
| Report received | 2021-05-17 |
| Manufacturer | KTM North America, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 171 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

HUSQVARNA 701 ENDURO LR 2020

## Defect

KTM North America, Inc. (KTM) is recalling all 2020 Husqvarna 701 Enduro LR motorcycles.  The fuel lines on the front fuel tank may chafe against the tank, possibly resulting in a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install two new fuel lines and an additional clamp, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 11, 2021.  Owners may contact KTM customer service at 1-888-985-6090.  KTM's number for this recall is HTB2105.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
