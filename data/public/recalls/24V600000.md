# NHTSA recall 24V600000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V600000 |
| Report received | 2024-08-12 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 89 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD VISION 2018, BLUE BIRD VISION 2019, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2020, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2021

## Defect

Blue Bird Bus Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2018-2021 Vision transit buses equipped with 6.8L gasoline engines.  The coating on the fuel lines may have been improperly applied, which can result in a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel lines as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 10, 2024.  Owners may contact Blue Bird customer service at 1-478-822-2242.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
