# NHTSA recall 24V303000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V303000 |
| Report received | 2024-04-29 |
| Manufacturer | Alliance RV, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 235 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ALLIANCE RV AVENUE 2024

## Defect

Alliance RV, LLC (Alliance) is recalling certain 2024 Avenue 22ML, 26RD, 29RL, 32RLS, 33RKS, 37MBR, and 38DBL travel trailers.  The inverter circuit was improperly wired to a 30 amp breaker, which could allow the breaker to overheat.

## Consequence

A breaker that overheats increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a 20 amp breaker, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 7, 2024.  Owners may contact Alliance customer service at 1-574-218-7165.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
