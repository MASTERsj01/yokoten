# NHTSA recall 26V520000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V520000 |
| Report received | 2026-08-10 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 92 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2023, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2024, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2025, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2026, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2027

## Defect

Blue Bird Body Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2023-2027 Vision Transit Buses. The 12V power wire on one of the harnesses may become damaged and short circuit.

## Consequence

An electrical short can cause a disruption to safety functions such as a loss of rear lighting or instrument panel warning lights, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will add an in-line fuse and repair the wire harnesses as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 9, 2026. Owners may contact Blue Bird customer service at 1-478-822-2242. Blue Bird's number for this recall is R26AG.
