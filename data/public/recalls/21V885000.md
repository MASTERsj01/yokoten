# NHTSA recall 21V885000: Steering:Hydraulic Power Assist:Pump

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V885000 |
| Report received | 2021-11-16 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:PUMP |
| Potential units affected | 44 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2021

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2021 Class A Cornerstone motorhomes.  A machining defect may cause pressure to build in the power steering pump and break the shaft seal, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.

## Consequence

A loss of power steering assist increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

A Spartan or Shyft Group repair center will inspect the pump serial number, and replace the power steering pump if necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed January 12, 2022.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
