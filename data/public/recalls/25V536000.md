# NHTSA recall 25V536000: Fuel System, Gasoline

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V536000 |
| Report received | 2025-08-21 |
| Manufacturer | General Motors, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE |
| Potential units affected | 23656 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CHEVROLET CORVETTE 2023, CHEVROLET CORVETTE 2024, CHEVROLET CORVETTE 2025, CHEVROLET CORVETTE CONVERTIBLE 2026, CHEVROLET CORVETTE COUPE 2026

## Defect

General Motors, LLC (GM) is recalling certain 2023â2026 Chevrolet Corvette vehicles equipped with a left-side radiator/fan combination. Excess fuel may spill into the vehicle fuel-filler pocket during refueling and leak onto an ignition source.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source, such as hot engine or exhaust components, can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a shield to divert spilled fuel, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 14, 2025. Owners may contact Chevrolet customer service at 1-800-222-1020. GM's number for this recall is N252514930.
