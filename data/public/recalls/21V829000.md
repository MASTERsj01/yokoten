# NHTSA recall 21V829000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V829000 |
| Report received | 2021-10-22 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY; ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 3 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER XLR TOYHAULER 2022

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2022 XLR Toy Hauler Recreational Vehicles.  The vehicles were incorrectly wired, and they are missing 250AMP over-current protection, which could result in melted wires and an electrical short.

## Consequence

An electrical short increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the gauge wire, and install a sub panel with the correct breaker, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed November 5, 2021.  Owners may contact Forest River Customer Service at 1-574-642-0432.  Forest River's number for this recall is 79-1429.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
