# NHTSA recall 23V442000: Fuel System, Diesel:Storage:Tank Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V442000 |
| Report received | 2023-06-23 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 2671 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR XSPOTTER 2022, AUTOCAR XSPOTTER 2023

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling certain 2022-2023 ACTT Xspotter vehicles.  The fuel tank mounting straps may not be properly secured.

## Consequence

An improperly secured fuel tank can separate from the vehicle, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and secure, or replace the fuel tank mounting straps as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed August 21, 2023.  Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611.  Autocar's number for this recall is ACTT-2302.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
