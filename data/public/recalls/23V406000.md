# NHTSA recall 23V406000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V406000 |
| Report received | 2023-06-05 |
| Manufacturer | Tiffin Motorhomes, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 450 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TIFFIN ALLEGRO BUS 2022, TIFFIN ALLEGRO BUS 2023, TIFFIN PHAETON 2022, TIFFIN PHAETON 2023, TIFFIN ZEPHYR 2022, TIFFIN ZEPHYR 2023

## Defect

Tiffin Motorhomes, Inc. (Tiffin) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Phaeton, Allegro Bus, and Zephyr motorhomes built on PowerGlide chassis.  A fuse will trip during an a/c compressor failure, causing a loss of power to the chassis.

## Consequence

Loss of power to the chassis can cause an engine stall, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect the chassis controller, and install a harness and inline fuse as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 20, 2023.  Owners may contact Tiffin customer service at 1-256-356-8661.  Tiffin's number for this recall is TIF-131.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
