# NHTSA recall 22V275000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V275000 |
| Report received | 2022-04-25 |
| Manufacturer | Blue Bird Body Company |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 8 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2022, BLUE BIRD VISION TRANSIT BUS 2023

## Defect

Blue Bird Body Company (Blue Bird) is recalling certain 2022-2023 Vision transit buses.  The fuel supply line was incorrectly routed, allowing it to chafe against a bolt head, which can damage the line and create a fuel leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will reroute the fuel line, and replace the fuel line if any damage is found, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed on June 21, 2022.  Owners may contact Blue Bird's customer service at 1-478-822-2242.  Blue Bird's number for this recall is R22BU-NSB.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
