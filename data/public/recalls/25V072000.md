# NHTSA recall 25V072000: Fuel System, Diesel:Storage:Tank Assembly

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V072000 |
| Report received | 2025-02-07 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:STORAGE:TANK ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 1106 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2021, ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2022, ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2023, ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2024, ENTEGRA LAUNCH 2025, ENTEGRA LAUNCH LE 2022, JAYCO TERRAIN 2021, JAYCO TERRAIN 2022, JAYCO TERRAIN 2023, JAYCO TERRAIN 2024, JAYCO TERRAIN 2025, JAYCO TERRAIN LE 2022

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2021-2025 Entegra Coach Launch, 2022 Launch LE, 2021-2025 Jayco Terrain, and 2022 Terrain LE recreational vehicles.  The grey water tank support brackets may contact and damage the fuel tank, causing a leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the support brackets, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed March 7, 2025.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number for this recall is 9903619.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
