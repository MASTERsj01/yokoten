# NHTSA recall 26V408000: Electrical System:Wiring:Switches/Knobs/Buttons

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V408000 |
| Report received | 2026-06-26 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:SWITCHES/KNOBS/BUTTONS |
| Potential units affected | 37 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX2 2026, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX2 2027

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain 2026-2027 Thomas Built SAF-T-LINER HDX2 transit buses. The entrance door may fail to open or close when the switch is activated.

## Consequence

A door that fails to open or close increases the risk of injury to occupants in a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the door relay for the entrance door system, free of charge.  Interim notification letters, notifying owners of the safety risk, were mailed August 25, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated October 2026. Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-574-0712. DTNA's number for this recall is F1035.
