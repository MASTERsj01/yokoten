# NHTSA recall 26V286000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Engine:Oil/Lubrication:Pipes, Hoses, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V286000 |
| Report received | 2026-05-06 |
| Manufacturer | Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:OIL/LUBRICATION:PIPES, HOSES, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 30 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2023, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2024, MICRO BIRD G5 TRANSIT BUS 2025

## Defect

Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. (Micro Bird) is recalling certain 2023-2025 G5 transit buses built on a GM chassis and equipped with a second air conditioning compressor kit. The drive belt pulley may contact and damage the the oil lines.

## Consequence

A damaged oil like may leak, increasing the risk of a fire in the presence of an ignition source.

## Remedy

Dealers will secure the oil lines, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed June 29, 2026. Owners can contact Micro Bird Customer Service at 819-477-2012. Micro Bird's number for this recall is 26-121-OUC.
