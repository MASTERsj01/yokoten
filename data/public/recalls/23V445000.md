# NHTSA recall 23V445000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Fuel Pump

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V445000 |
| Report received | 2023-06-26 |
| Manufacturer | Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:FUEL PUMP |
| Potential units affected | 143551 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2021, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2022, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C43 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 2021, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 S 2021, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 S 2022, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG C63 S 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG E53 2022, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG E53 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG E63 S 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG G63 2021, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG G63 2022 and others

## Defect

Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2021-2023 C-Class, E-Class, S-Class, CLS, SL, GT, GLC, GLE, GLS, and G-Class vehicles.  Please refer to MBUSA's recall report for specific vehicle details.  The fuel pump may shutdown, which can result in a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A loss of drive power can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel pump, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on June 14, 2024.  Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372.  MBUSA's number for this recall is 2023070012.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
