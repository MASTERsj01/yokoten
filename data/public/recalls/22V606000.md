# NHTSA recall 22V606000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Engine:Engine Control Module (Ecu/Ecm)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V606000 |
| Report received | 2022-08-09 |
| Manufacturer | Mack Trucks, Inc. |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:ENGINE CONTROL MODULE (ECU/ECM) |
| Potential units affected | 150 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MACK MD 2023

## Defect

Mack Trucks, Inc. (Mack) is recalling certain 2023 MD6/7 trucks.  The engine control module (ECM) may experience an internal short-circuit, which can result in an unexpected engine shut down.

## Consequence

An unexpected engine shut down can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Cummins will replace the ECM, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on September 27, 2022.  Owners may contact Mack's customer service at 1-800-866-1177.  Mack's number for this recall is SC0438.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
