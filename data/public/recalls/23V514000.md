# NHTSA recall 23V514000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V514000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-24 |
| Manufacturer | Mack Trucks, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 11 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MACK LRBE (ELECTRIC) 2022, MACK LRBE (ELECTRIC) 2023

## Defect

Mack Trucks, Inc. (Mack) is recalling certain 2022-2023 LR battery electric trucks.  The rivnut threaded inserts inside the high-voltage battery may be over-tightened and break, which could result in a short circuit.

## Consequence

An electrical short circuit increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the batteries, free of charge.  Owner letters were mailed November 1, 2023.  Owners may contact Mack customer service at 1-800-866-1177.  Mack's number for this recall is SC0447.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
