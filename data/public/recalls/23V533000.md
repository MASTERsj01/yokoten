# NHTSA recall 23V533000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V533000 |
| Report received | 2023-07-31 |
| Manufacturer | Farber Specialty Vehicles |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 1 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FARBER SPECIALTY FORD TRANSIT 2021

## Defect

Farber Specialty Vehicles (Farber) is recalling one 2021 Ford Transit van equipped with a Cummins Onan QG 2800i generator.  The fuel hose between the inline fuel filter and the Injection Pump Module (IPM) may have inadequate clamp force, possibly resulting in a gasoline leak.

## Consequence

A gasoline leak in the presence of an ignition source may increase the risk of fire.

## Remedy

A dealer will inspect and replace the fuel hose and clamps as necessary, free of charge.  The owner notification letters were mailed August 24, 2023.  Owners may contact Farber customer service at 1-800-331-3188.  Farber's number for this recall is PG5215.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
