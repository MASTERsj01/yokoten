# NHTSA recall 24V921000: Electrical System:Instrument Panel:Speedometer/Odometer

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V921000 |
| Report received | 2024-12-11 |
| Manufacturer | Gillig, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:INSTRUMENT PANEL:SPEEDOMETER/ODOMETER |
| Potential units affected | 9 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

GILLIG LOW FLOOR 2023

## Defect

Gillig, LLC (Gillig) is recalling certain 2023 Low Floor diesel-hybrid transit buses.  During vehicle operations in full electric vehicle (EV) mode, the odometer will not display accumulated mileage.  As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Controls and Displays."

## Consequence

A missing odometer reading could lead to an unintended delay in critical safety-related maintenance, potentially impairing the safe operation of the vehicle and increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Gillig will install a new speedometer gauge, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed December 20, 2024.  Owners may contact Gillig customer service at 1-800-735-1500 or 1-510-264-5073.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
