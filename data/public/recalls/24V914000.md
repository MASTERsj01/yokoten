# NHTSA recall 24V914000: Fuel System, Other

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V914000 |
| Report received | 2024-12-06 |
| Manufacturer | McNeilus Truck & Manufacturing, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER |
| Potential units affected | 15 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MCNEILUS ATLANTIC 2024, MCNEILUS ZERO RADIUS 2024

## Defect

McNeilus Truck & Manufacturing, Inc. (McNeilus) is recalling certain 2024 Atlantic and Zero Radius Side Loader vehicles equipped with certain Agility Fuel Solutions CNG Fuel Systems, using part number 10300466.  The manual cylinder valves may develop stress corrosion cracks in the bottom plugged port, causing a leak.

## Consequence

A natural gas leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Agility will replace the valves, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed January 17, 2025 by Agility.  Owners may contact Agility customer service at 1-844-474-7400 or McNeilus customer service at 1-888-686-7278.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
