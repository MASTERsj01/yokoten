# NHTSA recall 26V500000: Steering: Steering Wheel/Handle Bar

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V500000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-30 |
| Manufacturer | Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) |
| Component | STEERING: STEERING WHEEL/HANDLE BAR; STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS |
| Potential units affected | 43913 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

HONDA CMX300 2017, HONDA CMX300 2018, HONDA CMX300 2019, HONDA CMX300 2020, HONDA CMX300 2021, HONDA CMX300 2022, HONDA CMX300 2023, HONDA CMX300 2025, HONDA CMX300 2026, HONDA CMX500 2017, HONDA CMX500 2018, HONDA CMX500 2020 and others

## Defect

Honda (American Honda Motor Co.) is recalling certain 2017-2023, 2025 CMX500, 2017-2023, 2025-2026 CMX300, and 2025 Honda SCL500 motorcycles. The handlebar lock screw may loosen and detach.

## Consequence

A detached screw may lodge between the frame and stem, interfering with steering and increasing the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the lock screw and apply thread lock, free of charge. Interim letters notifying owners of the safety risk are expected to be mailed  September 21, 2026. Additional letters will be sent once the final remedy is available, anticipated in Winter 2026. Owners may contact Honda's customer service at 1-866-784-1870. Honda's number for this recall is KU8. This recall expands previous NHTSA recall number 26V146. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning August 4, 2026.
