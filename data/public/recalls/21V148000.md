# NHTSA recall 21V148000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Exhaust System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V148000 |
| Report received | 2021-03-09 |
| Manufacturer | Shyft Group |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:EXHAUST SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 34 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

UTILIMASTER MOBILE MAINTENANCE TRUCK 2017, UTILIMASTER MOBILE MAINTENANCE TRUCK 2018, UTILIMASTER MOBILE MAINTENANCE TRUCK 2019

## Defect

Shyft Group is recalling certain 2017-2019 Utilimaster Mobile Maintenance Trucks equipped with a generator set.  The flexible exhaust tube may tear, releasing hot exhaust gases into the generator compartment.

## Consequence

An exhaust gas leak in the generator compartment can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Shyft Group will notify owners, and dealers will replace the exhaust tube, free of charge.  The recall began on March 26, 2021.  Owners may contact Shyft Group customer service at 1-800-582-3454.  Shyft Group's number for this recall is 21-04.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
