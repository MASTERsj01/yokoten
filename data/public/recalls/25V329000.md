# NHTSA recall 25V329000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V329000 |
| Report received | 2025-06-02 |
| Manufacturer | Custom Truck And Body Works |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 3 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

CUSTOM TRUCK & BODY WORKS FORD F-550 2020

## Defect

Custom Truck and Body Works (Custom Truck) is recalling certain 2020 Ford F-550 vehicles equipped with Weldon 8X16 Input-Output nodes. In the event of a short circuit or wiring failure, the firmware in the node may fail to shut off the output pin, resulting in overloaded electrical circuits.

## Consequence

Overloaded electrical circuits can overheat, increasing the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Custom Truck will work with Weldon to update the firmware, free of charge. All vehicles were repaired.  Owners may contact Custom Truck customer service at 1-888-712-2963 or Weldon Division of Akron Brass customer service at 1-800-228-1161.
