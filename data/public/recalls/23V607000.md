# NHTSA recall 23V607000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V607000 |
| Report received | 2023-08-31 |
| Manufacturer | Nova Bus (US) Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 1189 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NOVA BUS LFS 2017, NOVA BUS LFS 2018, NOVA BUS LFS 2019, NOVA BUS LFS 2020, NOVA BUS LFS 2021, NOVA BUS LFS 2022, NOVA BUS LFS 2023, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2017, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2018, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2019, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2020, NOVA BUS LFS ARTIC 2021 and others

## Defect

Nova Bus (US) Inc. (Nova) is recalling certain 2017-2023 LFS and 2017-2022 LFS Artic transit buses.  The 24-volt power cable and grounded mounting fastener that secures the fuse holder may short circuit.

## Consequence

An electrical short circuit can overheat the fuse holder and increase the risk of a fire while driving or parked.

## Remedy

Nova will insect, remove, and clean the fuse holder and cable lugs as necessary, free of charge.  Owner letters were mailed November 7, 2023.  Owners may contact Nova customer service at 1-800-350-6682.  Nova's number for this recall is CR5445.  This recall replaces and expands recall number 22V-416.  Vehicles previously repaired under recall 22V-416 will need to have the new remedy completed.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
