# NHTSA recall 22V548000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V548000 |
| Report received | 2022-07-28 |
| Manufacturer | Gillig, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 184 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

GILLIG LOW FLOOR 2019, GILLIG LOW FLOOR 2020, GILLIG LOW FLOOR 2021, GILLIG LOW FLOOR 2022

## Defect

Gillig, LLC (Gillig) is recalling certain 2019-2022 Low Floor vehicles equipped with Cummins BP74E battery packs.  The battery packs may develop a coolant leak internally, causing the battery pack to short circuit.

## Consequence

A battery short circuit increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Gillig will tighten the coolant fittings and replace the coolant, free of charge.  Owner notification were mailed on August 16, 2022.  Owners may contact Gillig's customer service at 1-510-264-5073 or 1-800-735-1500.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
