# NHTSA recall 26V226000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V226000 |
| Report received | 2026-04-09 |
| Manufacturer | International Motors, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY; ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:SWITCHES/KNOBS/BUTTONS |
| Potential units affected | 1896 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

IC BUS EVSB 2022, IC BUS EVSB 2023, IC BUS EVSB 2024, IC BUS EVSB 2025, IC BUS EVSB 2026, IC BUS EVSB 2027

## Defect

International Motors, LLC (International) is recalling certain 2022-2027 IC Bus EVSB school buses. The high voltage disconnect switch may fail to shut off power to the battery.

## Consequence

Switch failure may lead to unintentionally working on an energized high voltage battery, increasing the risk of injury or death by electrical shock.

## Remedy

Dealers will repair the high voltage disconnect connector bypass circuit, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 4, 2026. Owners may contact International's customer service at 1-800-448-7825. International's number for this recall is 26505. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning June 1, 2026.
