# NHTSA recall 24V254000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V254000 |
| Report received | 2024-04-05 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 1552 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

COACHMEN CATALINA 2024, FOREST RIVER AURORA 2024

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2024 Aurora and Coachmen Catalina Travel Trailers.  Improper routing may leave the distribution panel wire unprotected from the battery.

## Consequence

An unprotected wire increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will reroute the distribution panel wire to the breaker side of the mini breaker, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 26, 2024.  Owners may contact Forest River Customer Service at 1-574-825-4995.  Forest River's number for this recall is 203-1757.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
