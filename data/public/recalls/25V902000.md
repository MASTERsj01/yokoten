# NHTSA recall 25V902000: Steering:Hydraulic Power Assist:Pump

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V902000 |
| Report received | 2025-12-19 |
| Manufacturer | Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:PUMP |
| Potential units affected | 10 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICRO BIRD G5e TRANSIT BUS 2018, MICRO BIRD G5e TRANSIT BUS 2019, MICRO BIRD G5e TRANSIT BUS 2024, MICRO BIRD G5e TRANSIT BUS 2025, MICRO BIRD G5e TRANSIT BUS 2026

## Defect

Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. (Micro Bird) is recalling certain 2018-2019, 2024-2026 G5E transit buses. The power steering pump may fail to start when the ignition is turned on or may shutdown unexpectedly.

## Consequence

A loss of power steering assist can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and update the software as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed February 18, 2026. Owners can contact Micro Bird Customer Service at 819-477-2012. Micro Bird's number for this recall is 25-119-PUC.
