# NHTSA recall 24V297000: Steering:Hydraulic Power Assist:Hose, Piping, And Connections

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V297000 |
| Report received | 2024-04-26 |
| Manufacturer | Corp. Micro Bird, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:HOSE, PIPING, AND CONNECTIONS |
| Potential units affected | 89 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2019, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2020, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2021, MICRO BIRD G5 SCHOOL BUS 2022

## Defect

Corp. Mirco Bird, Inc. (Micro Bird) is recalling certain 2019-2022 G5 school buses.  An inadequate connection between the power steering pressure line and the brake hydroboost unit may result in a sudden loss of power steering fluid.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of power steering fluid can result in a loss of power steering and power brake assist, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the power steering lines as necessary, free of charge.  Vehicles in this recall were previously recalled under 22V-811 and owners will need to have the new remedy completed even if their vehicle was remedied under the prior recall.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 10, 2024.  Owners may contact Mirco Bird customer service at 1-819-477-2012.  Micro Bird's number for this recall is 24-107-FUS.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
