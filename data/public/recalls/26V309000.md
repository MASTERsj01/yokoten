# NHTSA recall 26V309000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Inverter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V309000 |
| Report received | 2026-05-13 |
| Manufacturer | Lucid USA, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:INVERTER |
| Potential units affected | 2039 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

LUCID AIR 2024, LUCID AIR 2025

## Defect

Lucid USA, Inc. (Lucid) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Air vehicles equipped with rear-wheel drive. The Gen 4 inverter may experience internal friction and become damaged, causing a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Lucid will release an over-the-air (OTA) software update to continually inspect for an inverter failure. If a failure is detected Lucid will replace the inverter, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed July 8, 2026. Owners may contact Lucid at 1-888-995-8243. Lucid's number for this recall is SR-26-05-0.
