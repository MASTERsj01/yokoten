# NHTSA recall 24V372000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Battery:Management System/Energy Control Module (Bms/Becm):Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V372000 |
| Report received | 2024-05-28 |
| Manufacturer | Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION BATTERY:MANAGEMENT SYSTEM/ENERGY CONTROL MODULE (BMS/BECM):SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 14912 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MERCEDES-BENZ AMG EQE 53 4MATIC 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG EQE SUV 53 4MATIC 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG EQE SUV 53 4MATIC 2025, MERCEDES-BENZ AMG EQS 53 4MATIC 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE 350 4MATIC 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE 350+ 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE 500 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE SUV 350 4MATIC 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE SUV 350 4MATIC 2024, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE SUV 350 4MATIC 2025, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE SUV 350+ 2023, MERCEDES-BENZ EQE SUV 350+ 2024 and others

## Defect

Mercedes-Benz USA, LLC (MBUSA) is recalling certain 2024 EQE 350 4MATIC, EQE 350+, EQE 500 4MATIC, EQS 450 4MATIC, EQS 450+, EQS 580 4MATIC, AMG EQE 53 4MATIC, AMG EQS 53 4MATIC, 2023-2025 EQE SUV 350 4MATIC, EQE SUV 350+, EQE SUV 500 4MATIC, EQS SUV 450 4MATIC, EQS SUV 450+, EQS SUV 580 4MATIC, 2024-2025 EQS SUV 680 4MATIC, and AMG EQE SUV 53 4MATIC vehicles.  The battery management system software may cause the high-voltage battery to shut down, which can result in a sudden loss of drive power.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the battery management system software, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 5, 2024.  Owners may contact MBUSA customer service at 1-800-367-6372.  MBUSA's number for this recall is 2024060002.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
