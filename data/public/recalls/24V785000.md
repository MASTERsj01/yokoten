# NHTSA recall 24V785000: Electrical System:Wiring:Connectors/Plugs/Receptacles

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V785000 |
| Report received | 2024-10-21 |
| Manufacturer | Triumph Motorcycles America, Ltd. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:CONNECTORS/PLUGS/RECEPTACLES |
| Potential units affected | 8801 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T100 2022, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T100 2023, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T100 2024, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T120 2022, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T120 2023, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T120 2024, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T120 BLACK 2022, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T120 BLACK 2023, TRIUMPH BONNEVILLE T120 BLACK 2024, TRIUMPH SCRAMBLER 900 2023, TRIUMPH SCRAMBLER 900 2024, TRIUMPH SPEED TWIN 900 2023 and others

## Defect

Triumph Motorcycles America, Ltd. (Triumph) is recalling certain 2022-2024 Bonneville T100, T120, T120 Black, 2022 Triumph Street Scrambler, Street Scrambler Sandstorm, Street Twin, Street Twin Goldline, 2023-2024 Scrambler 900, and Speed Twin 900 motorcycles.  The wiring between the alternator and main harness connector may short circuit and overheat.

## Consequence

Overheated wiring increases the risk of a fire or crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will install a new wire alignment clip, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed November 18, 2024.  Owners may contact Triumph customer service at 1-678-854-2010.  Triumph's number for this recall is SRAN 627.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
