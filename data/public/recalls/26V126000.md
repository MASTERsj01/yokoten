# NHTSA recall 26V126000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Traction Motor:Controller

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V126000 |
| Report received | 2026-03-05 |
| Manufacturer | Mitsubishi Fuso Truck of America, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:TRACTION MOTOR:CONTROLLER; ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 244 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MITSUBISHI FUSO RIZON FEC7K (E16) 2024, MITSUBISHI FUSO RIZON FEC9K (E18) 2024, MITSUBISHI FUSO RIZON FECXK (E18) 2025

## Defect

Mitsubishi Fuso Truck of America, Inc. (MFTA) is recalling certain 2024 Rizon FEC7K (E16), FEC9K (E18), and 2025 FECXK (E18) trucks. While idling, the electrical control unit may transmit an error that prevents the truck from being driven.

## Consequence

A vehicle that is unable to move as needed, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will reprogram battery management system and DC-Box software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed March 30, 2026. Owners may contact MFTA customer service at 1-877-711-0707. MFTA's number for this recall is C10136. Vehicle Identification Numbers (VINs) involved in this recall will be searchable on NHTSA.gov beginning March 16, 2026.
