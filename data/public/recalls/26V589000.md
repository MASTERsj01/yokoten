# NHTSA recall 26V589000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V589000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-11 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 155 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER ROCKPORT 2024, FOREST RIVER ROCKPORT 2025, FOREST RIVER ROCKPORT 2026

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2024-2026 Rockport trucks. Some wiring may have been incorrectly routed. The underside of the hood may contact and damage the wires, causing a short circuit.

## Consequence

A short circuit increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will reroute the wiring, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 19, 2026. Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-574-522-7599. Forest River's number for this recall is 51-2096.
