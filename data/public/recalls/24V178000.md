# NHTSA recall 24V178000: Steering:Column

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V178000 |
| Report received | 2024-03-06 |
| Manufacturer | Prevost Car (US) Inc. |
| Component | STEERING:COLUMN |
| Potential units affected | 23 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

PREVOST H3-45 2023, PREVOST H3-45 VIP 2022, PREVOST H3-45 VIP 2023

## Defect

Prevost Car (US) Inc. (Prevost) is recalling certain 2023 H3-45 and 2022-2023 H3-45 VIP vehicles.  The steering column joints may fail, causing a loss of steering control.

## Consequence

A loss of steering control increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Owners are advised not to drive their vehicle until the repair has been performed.  Prevost will replace the steering column joints, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed March 19, 2024.  Owners may contact Provost customer service at 1-866-870-2046.  Prevost's number for this recall is SR24-11.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
