# NHTSA recall 21V105000: Steering:Hydraulic Power Assist:Hose, Piping, And Connections

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V105000 |
| Report received | 2021-02-23 |
| Manufacturer | Seagrave Fire Apparatus, LLC |
| Component | STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:HOSE, PIPING, AND CONNECTIONS; STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST:POWER STEERING FLUID |
| Potential units affected | 54 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2019, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2020, SEAGRAVE ATTACKER 2021, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2019, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2020, SEAGRAVE CAPITAL 2021

## Defect

Seagrave Fire Apparatus, LLC (Seagrave) is recalling certain 2019-2021 Attacker and Capitol vehicles equipped with a Cummins X12 engine.  The spin-on power steering filter may not have been tightened correctly, which can cause it to loosen and leak power steering fluid.

## Consequence

A power steering fluid leak may result in the loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Seagrave will inspect the power steering filter, and retighten or replace it as necessary, free of charge.  Owners may contact Seagrave customer service at 1-800-777-5360.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
