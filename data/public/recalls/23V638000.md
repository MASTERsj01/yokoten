# NHTSA recall 23V638000: Electrical System:12V/24V/48V Battery

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V638000 |
| Report received | 2023-09-15 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:12V/24V/48V BATTERY |
| Potential units affected | 39 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER PALOMINO 2024, PALOMINO REAL-LITE CAMPER 2024

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2024 Palomino hardside, softside, and real-lite truck campers.  An unnecessary charge line may have been connected to the lithium battery.

## Consequence

The charge line may overcharge the lithium battery, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will disconnect the charge line and install bell caps, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 25, 2023.  Owners may contact Palomino customer service at 1-269-432-3271.  Forest River's number for this recall is 400-1677.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
