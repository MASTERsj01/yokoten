# NHTSA recall 24V081000: Power Train:Driveline:Chain/Belt

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V081000 |
| Report received | 2024-02-07 |
| Manufacturer | Suzuki Motor USA, LLC |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:DRIVELINE:CHAIN/BELT |
| Potential units affected | 360 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

SUZUKI GSX-8R 2024

## Defect

Suzuki Motor USA, LLC (Suzuki) is recalling certain 2024 GSX-8R motorcycles.  The drive chain may detach due to missing rivets, which can result in a loss of power to the rear wheels.

## Consequence

A loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and repair the drive chain with a replacement joint and replace sprockets as necessary, free of charge.
