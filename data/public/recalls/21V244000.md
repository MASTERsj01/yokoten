# NHTSA recall 21V244000: Electrical System:Propulsion System:Inverter

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V244000 |
| Report received | 2021-04-07 |
| Manufacturer | New Flyer of America, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM:INVERTER |
| Potential units affected | 227 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NEW FLYER XE35 2019, NEW FLYER XE35 2020, NEW FLYER XE35 2021, NEW FLYER XE40 2013, NEW FLYER XE40 2014, NEW FLYER XE40 2015, NEW FLYER XE40 2016, NEW FLYER XE40 2017, NEW FLYER XE40 2018, NEW FLYER XE40 2019, NEW FLYER XE40 2020, NEW FLYER XE40 2021 and others

## Defect

New Flyer of America, Inc. (New Flyer) is recalling certain 2013-2021 XE40, 2019-2021 XE35, 2018-2021 XE60, 2016-2019 XHE40, and 2015 XHE60 vehicles.  Firmware errors in the inverters may cause the electric drive system to shutdown.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

New Flyer will notify owners, and dealers will update firmware software, free of charge.  The recall began on April 19, 2021.  Owners may contact New Flyer customer service at 1-204-224-6706.  New Flyer's number for this recall is R21-005.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.safercar.gov.
