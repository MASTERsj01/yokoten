# NHTSA recall 24V222000: Electrical System:Wiring:Fuses And Circuit Breakers

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V222000 |
| Report received | 2024-03-22 |
| Manufacturer | Reading Truck Equipment LLC |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING:FUSES AND CIRCUIT BREAKERS |
| Potential units affected | 48 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

READING TRUCK EQUIPMENT DUMP BODIED TRUCKS 2021, READING TRUCK EQUIPMENT DUMP BODIED TRUCKS 2022, READING TRUCK EQUIPMENT DUMP BODIED TRUCKS 2023, READING TRUCK EQUIPMENT DUMP BODIED TRUCKS 2024

## Defect

Reading Truck Equipment, LLC (Reading) is recalling certain 2021-2024 dump body trucks.  Inadequate clearance may allow the hood to contact the main electrical fuse terminal.

## Consequence

Contact with the main fuse terminal can cause electrical arcing, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and relocate fuse terminal and fuse, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 25, 2024.  Owners may contact Reading customer service at 1-800-458-2226.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
