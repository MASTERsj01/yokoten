# NHTSA recall 22V254000: Power Train:Automatic Transmission:Gear Position Indication (Prndl)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V254000 |
| Report received | 2022-04-14 |
| Manufacturer | Ford Motor Company |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION:GEAR POSITION INDICATION (PRNDL); POWER TRAIN:SHIFT LINKAGE/CABLE/ROD |
| Potential units affected | 96015 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FORD ESCAPE 2015

## Defect

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2015 Escape vehicles equipped with 2.0L engines.  The bushing that attaches the shifter cable to the transmission may degrade or detach.

## Consequence

A damaged or missing bushing may prevent the vehicle from shifting into the intended gear, and cause the vehicle to move in an unexpected direction.  Additionally, the vehicle may roll after the driver selects the 'Park' position.  Either scenario increases the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the under hood shifter bushing and add a protective cap over the shift cable bushing, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2022.  Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.  Ford's number for this recall is 22S25.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
