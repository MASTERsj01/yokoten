# NHTSA recall 25V604000: Steering:Critical Fasteners

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V604000 |
| Report received | 2025-09-11 |
| Manufacturer | Daimler Trucks North America, LLC |
| Component | STEERING:CRITICAL FASTENERS; STEERING:LINKAGES:TIE ROD ASSEMBLY |
| Potential units affected | 99 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2025, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 2026, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER C2 JOULEY 2026, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER EFX 2026, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX 2025, THOMAS BUILT BUSES SAF-T-LINER HDX2 2026

## Defect

Daimler Trucks North America, LLC (DTNA) is recalling certain Thomas Built Buses 2025-2026 Saf-T-Liner HDX, Saf-T-Liner C2, 2026 Saf-T-Liner EFX, and Saf-T-Liner C2 Jouley school buses. The tie rod castle nut and cotter pin may not be installed properly, which can result in a loss of vehicle steering control.

## Consequence

A loss of vehicle steering control increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the tie rod assembly, as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed November 10, 2025. Owners may contact DTNA customer service at 1-800-547-0712. DTNA's number for this recall is D25R2.
