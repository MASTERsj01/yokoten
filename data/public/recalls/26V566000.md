# NHTSA recall 26V566000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V566000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-01 |
| Manufacturer | Storyteller Overland |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 169 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

STORYTELLER OVERLAND GXV HILT 2026, STORYTELLER OVERLAND MODE VAN 2026, STORYTELLER OVERLAND MODE VAN 2027

## Defect

Storyteller Overland, LLC (Storyteller) is recalling certain 2026-2027 MODE and 2026 GXV HILTS vehicles equipped with certain MaxxAir N-Series Maxxfan rooftop ventilation fans. The printed circuit board may fail during certain operations and overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated printed circuit board increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Storyteller will inspect and replace the printed circuit board as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed October 30, 2026. Owners may contact Storyteller customer service at 1-888-999-7442.
