# NHTSA recall 26V039000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V039000 |
| Report received | 2026-01-23 |
| Manufacturer | Forest River, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 247 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FOREST RIVER CAMPSITE RESERVE 2026, FOREST RIVER CASCADE 2026, FOREST RIVER CHEROKEE 2026

## Defect

Forest River, Inc. (Forest River) is recalling certain 2026 Cascade, Cherokee, and Campsite Reserve recreational trailers. The 7-way connector may have been incorrectly wired to a breaker without over-current protection.

## Consequence

Without over-current protection, a short circuit can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will correct the wiring, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed March 4, 2026. Owners may contact Forest River customer service at 1-260-499-2100. Forest River's number for this recall is 95-2019.
