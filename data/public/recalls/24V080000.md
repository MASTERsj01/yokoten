# NHTSA recall 24V080000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V080000 |
| Report received | 2024-04-05 |
| Manufacturer | Autocar, LLC |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 37 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

AUTOCAR XPEDITOR 2023, AUTOCAR XSPOTTER 2023

## Defect

Autocar, LLC (Autocar) is recalling certain 2023 ACX (Xpeditor) and ACTT (Xspotter) vehicles.  The steering gear assemblies may have been assembled incorrectly with fewer recirculating balls than are required, which may result in a loss of steering control.

## Consequence

A loss of steering control increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering gears as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 4, 2024.  Owners may contact Autocar customer service at 1-888-218-3611.  Autocar's numbers for this recall are ACX-2401 and ACTT-2401.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
