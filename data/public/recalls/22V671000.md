| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V671000 |
| Report received | 2022-09-07 |
| Manufacturer | Nissan North America, Inc. |
| Component | POWER TRAIN:AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION |
| Potential units affected | 197230 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NISSAN FRONTIER 2020, NISSAN FRONTIER 2021, NISSAN FRONTIER 2022, NISSAN TITAN 2020, NISSAN TITAN 2021, NISSAN TITAN 2022, NISSAN TITAN 2023

## Defect

Nissan North America, Inc. (Nissan) is recalling certain 2020-2023 Titan, 2020-2022 Frontier, and 2023 Z vehicles.  The transmission parking pawl may not engage when the vehicle is shifted into park, which can result in a vehicle rollaway.

## Consequence

A vehicle rollaway increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Owners are advised to apply the parking brake every time they park their vehicle.
