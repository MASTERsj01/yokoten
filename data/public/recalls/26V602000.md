# NHTSA recall 26V602000: Electrical System:Adas:Control Module

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V602000 |
| Report received | 2026-09-18 |
| Manufacturer | REV Recreation Group |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ADAS:CONTROL MODULE |
| Potential units affected | 47 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FLEETWOOD PALISADE 2026

## Defect

REV Recreation Group (REV) is recalling certain 2025-2026 American Coach American Dream, 2026-2027 Fleetwood Discovery LXE, 2026 Fleetwood Palisade, and Holiday Rambler Armada recreational vehicles. The RoadWatch Safety System may have been programmed with the incorrect vehicle parameters.

## Consequence

Safety functions that depend on RoadWatch, such as electronic stability control (ESC), may have diminished or lost functionality, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and reprogram RoadWatch as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed November 16, 2026. Owners may contact REV customer service at 1-800-509-3417. REV's number for this recall is 260911REV.
