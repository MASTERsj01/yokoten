# NHTSA recall 25V749000: Fuel System, Other

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V749000 |
| Report received | 2025-10-31 |
| Manufacturer | The Heil Co. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER |
| Potential units affected | 50 |
| Recall initiated by | ODI |

## Vehicles

HEIL  SIDE LOADER GARBAGE TRUCK 2023, HEIL  SIDE LOADER GARBAGE TRUCK 2024, HEIL  SIDE LOADER GARBAGE TRUCK 2025

## Defect

The Heil Co. (Heil) is recalling certain 2023-2025 side loader garbage trucks equipped with certain Agility Fuel Solutions CNG Fuel Systems, using part number 10300466. The manual cylinder valves may develop stress corrosion cracks in the bottom plugged port, causing a leak.

## Consequence

A natural gas leak in the presence of an ignition source increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Agility will replace the valves, free of charge. All affected garbage trucks were repaired by August 14, 2025. Owners may contact Agility's customer service at 1-844-474-7400 or Heil's customer service at 1-866-275-4345.
