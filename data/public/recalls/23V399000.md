# NHTSA recall 23V399000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V399000 |
| Report received | 2023-06-02 |
| Manufacturer | Triple E Recreational Vehicles |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 24 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TRIPLE E RV WONDER 2023, TRIPLE E UNITY 2023

## Defect

Triple E Recreational Vehicles (Triple E) is recalling certain 2023 Unity U24MBL and Wonder W24MBL motorhomes.  The cable that connects the air conditioning to the junction box may have been cut during installation, exposing the wire to the metal junction box.

## Consequence

Exposed wire can cause a spark and increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will shorten the wiring at the point of the cut, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed June 16, 2023.  Owners may contact Triple E customer service at 1-877-992-9906.  Triple E's number for this recall is CA#10323-1.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
