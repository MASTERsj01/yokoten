# NHTSA recall 24V342000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V342000 |
| Report received | 2024-05-15 |
| Manufacturer | Alamo Group Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 26 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2023, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1821 2024, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1922 2023, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 1922 2024, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2023, MORBARK EEGER BEEVER 2131 2024

## Defect

Alamo Group Inc. (Morbark) is recalling certain 2023-2024 Morbark EB1821, EB2131, and EB1922 brush chippers.  The fuel lines that provide fuel to the engine may not have the necessary pressure rating of 58 pounds per square inch (PSI).

## Consequence

An engine fuel line rated below the necessary pressure rating may burst and leak fuel, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the engine fuel lines, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed July 9, 2024.   Owners may contact Morbark's customer service at 1-800-255-8839.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
