# NHTSA recall 23V829000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Engine:Crank/Camshaft Position Sensor

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V829000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-07 |
| Manufacturer | Indian Motorcycle Company |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:CRANK/CAMSHAFT POSITION SENSOR |
| Potential units affected | 1215 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

INDIAN CHIEFTAIN 2023, INDIAN CHIEFTAIN 2024, INDIAN CHIEFTAIN DARK HORSE 2023, INDIAN CHIEFTAIN DARK HORSE 2024, INDIAN CHIEFTAIN ELITE 2023, INDIAN CHIEFTAIN LIMITED 2023, INDIAN CHIEFTAIN LIMITED 2024, INDIAN ROADMASTER 2023, INDIAN ROADMASTER 2024, INDIAN ROADMASTER DARK HORSE 2023, INDIAN ROADMASTER DARK HORSE 2024, INDIAN ROADMASTER ELITE 2024 and others

## Defect

Indian Motorcycle Company (Indian) is recalling certain 2023 Chieftain Elite, 2023-2024 Chieftain Dark Horse, Chieftain Dark Horse Icon, Chieftain Limited, Chieftain, Roadmaster Limited, Roadmaster Dark Horse, Roadmaster, Springfield, Springfield Dark Horse, and 2024 Roadmaster Elite motorcycles. The crankshaft position sensor may have been routed improperly, which can result in an engine stall.

## Consequence

An engine stall can increase the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and reroute, or replace the crankshaft position sensor as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed December 22, 2023.  Owners may contact Indian customer service at 1-877-204-3697.  Indian's number for this recall is I-23-07.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
