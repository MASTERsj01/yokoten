# NHTSA recall 22V179000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V179000 |
| Report received | 2022-03-24 |
| Manufacturer | Prevost Car (US) Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 140 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

PREVOST X3-45 COMMUTER 2021, PREVOST X3-45 COMMUTER 2022

## Defect

Prevost Car (US) Inc. (Prevost) is recalling certain 2021-2022 X3-45 Com coach buses.  A nut located on the rear junction box may have been improperly tightened, causing a cable to loosen.

## Consequence

A loose junction box cable may overheat, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Prevost will properly tighten the cables on the rear junction box, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed on May 20, 2022.  Owners may contact Prevost's customer service at 1-866-870-2046.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
