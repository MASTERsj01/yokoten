# NHTSA recall 26V555000: Electrical System: Instrument Cluster/Panel

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V555000 |
| Report received | 2026-08-31 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM: INSTRUMENT CLUSTER/PANEL |
| Potential units affected | 11 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA QWEST 2027, JAYCO MELBOURNE PRESTIGE 2027

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2027 Entegra Qwest and Jayco Melbourne Prestige recreational vehicles. The taillight harness may have been manufactured with an incorrect resistor. The defective harness may cause the warning light to illuminate on the instrument panel display when the taillights are functioning correctly. As such, these vehicles fail to comply with the requirements of Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard (FMVSS) number 101, "Control and Displays."

## Consequence

The incorrect warning light may fail to alert the driver to critical safety information or cause confusion, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the taillight harness, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed September 30, 2026. Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267. Jayco's number for this recall is 9903667.
