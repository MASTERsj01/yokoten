# NHTSA recall 26V450000: Electrical System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V450000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-14 |
| Manufacturer | Winnebago Industries, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 134 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

WINNEBAGO NAVION 2027, WINNEBAGO VIEW 2027

## Defect

Winnebago Industries, Inc. (Winnebago) is recalling certain 2027 Navion and View recreational vehicles equipped with certain MaxxAir N-Series Maxxfan rooftop ventilation fans. The printed circuit board may fail during certain operations and overheat.

## Consequence

An overheated printed circuit board increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the printed circuit board as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed September 4, 2026. Owners may contact Winnebago customer service at 1-641-585-6939 or 1-800-537-1885. Winnebago's number for this recall is 209.
