# NHTSA recall 25V588000: Electrical System:Alternator/Generator/Regulator:Integrated Starter(Other Than Propulsion)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V588000 |
| Report received | 2025-09-09 |
| Manufacturer | Storyteller Overland |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:ALTERNATOR/GENERATOR/REGULATOR:INTEGRATED STARTER(OTHER THAN PROPULSION); ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 61 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

STORYTELLER OVERLAND GXV HILT 2024, STORYTELLER OVERLAND GXV HILT 2025, STORYTELLER OVERLAND GXV HILT 2026

## Defect

Storyteller Overland, LLC (Storyteller) is recalling certain 2024-2026 GXV Hilt recreational vehicles. The auxiliary power system wires may have been improperly. The wires may be damaged by contact with the intake manifold.

## Consequence

Damaged wire may overheat and short, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect, replace, and secure the wires as necessary, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed October 8, 2025. Owners may contact Storyteller customer service at 1-888-999-7442.
