# NHTSA recall 25V497000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 25V497000 |
| Report received | 2025-07-30 |
| Manufacturer | Brinkley RV |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 35 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BRINKLEY RV MODEL Z 2025

## Defect

Brinkley RV (Brinkley) is recalling certain 2025 Model Z trailers. The electrical wiring may not be secured properly, which can result in damaged wiring and a short circuit.

## Consequence

A short circuit increases the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will secure the electrical wires, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 14, 2025. Owners may contact Brinkley customer service at 1-574-501-4280.  Brinkley's number for this recall is REC-2503.
