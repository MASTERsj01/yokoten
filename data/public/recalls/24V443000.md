# NHTSA recall 24V443000: Electrical System:Body Control Module:Software

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V443000 |
| Report received | 2024-06-14 |
| Manufacturer | Ford Motor Company |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:BODY CONTROL MODULE:SOFTWARE |
| Potential units affected | 159 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FORD F-150 2021

## Defect

Ford Motor Company (Ford) is recalling certain 2021 F-150 vehicles equipped with the Onboard Scales feature.  An over-the-air software update to the body control module (BCM) may have disabled both reverse lights.

## Consequence

Disabled reverse lights cannot alert others that the vehicle is reversing, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the body control module software, free of charge.  Owner notification letters are were mailed June 26, 2024.   Owners may contact Ford customer service at 1-866-436-7332.  Ford's number for this recall is 24S38.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
