# NHTSA recall 24V161000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V161000 |
| Report received | 2024-02-29 |
| Manufacturer | Jayco, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 286 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2008, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2024, ENTEGRA ANTHEM 2025, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2023, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2024, ENTEGRA CORNERSTONE 2025

## Defect

Jayco, Inc. (Jayco) is recalling certain 2023-2025 Entegra Coach Anthem and Cornerstone motorhomes.  The steering gear may have foreign material inside the gear that could build pressure within the system, resulting in a loss of power steering assist.

## Consequence

A sudden loss of power steering assist can increase the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will remove and replace the steering gear, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed April 10, 2024.  Owners may contact Jayco customer service at 1-800-283-8267.  Jayco's number for this recall is Shyft Group 24V-052.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
