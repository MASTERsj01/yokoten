# NHTSA recall 26V285000: Fuel System, Other:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V285000 |
| Report received | 2026-05-05 |
| Manufacturer | Winnebago Industries, Inc. |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, OTHER:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 155 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

WINNEBAGO SOLIS 2026, WINNEBAGO SOLIS 2027

## Defect

Winnebago Industries, Inc. (Winnebago) is recalling certain 2026-2027 Solis recreational vehicles. The liquid propane (LP) hose may be incorrectly routed over the exhaust heat shield.

## Consequence

Heat exposure may damage the hose. A damaged hose may leak fuel, increasing the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will reroute the LP hose, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed May 27, 2026. Owners may contact Winnebago customer service at 1-641-585-6939 or 1-800-537-1885. Winnebago's number for this recall is 206.
