# NHTSA recall 21V920000: Steering:Hydraulic Power Assist System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 21V920000 |
| Report received | 2021-11-23 |
| Manufacturer | Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing |
| Component | STEERING:HYDRAULIC POWER ASSIST SYSTEM; STEERING:RACK AND PINION |
| Potential units affected | 22462 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2008, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2009, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2010, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2011, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2012, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2013, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2014, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2015, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2016, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2017, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2018, TOYOTA SEQUOIA 2019 and others

## Defect

Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2008-2022 Sequoia and 2007-2021 Tundra vehicles.  The power steering gear assembly may have been manufactured incorrectly, which can result in an oil leak.

## Consequence

An oil leak may cause a sudden loss of power steering assist, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the power steering gear assembly as necessary, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on January 21, 2022.  Owners may contact Toyota customer service at 1-800-331-4331.  Toyota's numbers for this recall are 21TB10 and 21TA10.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
