# NHTSA recall 23V819000: Electrical System:Wiring

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 23V819000 |
| Report received | 2023-12-06 |
| Manufacturer | Ferrara Fire Apparatus, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 6 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

FERRARA INFERNO 2018, FERRARA INFERNO 2019, FERRARA INFERNO 2020

## Defect

Ferrara Fire apparatus, Inc. (Ferrara) is recalling certain 2018-2020 Inferno Aerial Fire Apparatus trucks.  In the event of a short circuit or wiring failure, the firmware in the vehicle multiplex system may fail to shut off the output pin, resulting in overloaded electrical circuits.

## Consequence

Overloaded electrical circuits can overheat, increasing the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Ferrara will work with the equipment manufacturer to update the firmware, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed January 26, 2024.  Owners may contact Ferrara customer service at 1-800-443-9006.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
