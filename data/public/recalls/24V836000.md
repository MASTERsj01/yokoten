# NHTSA recall 24V836000: Electrical System:Propulsion System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 24V836000 |
| Report received | 2024-11-05 |
| Manufacturer | Lucid USA, Inc. |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM; ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:WIRING |
| Potential units affected | 1539 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

LUCID AIR 2024, LUCID AIR 2025

## Defect

Lucid USA, Inc. (Lucid) is recalling certain 2024-2025 Air Pure rear wheel drive vehicles.  The rear subframe wiring harness may be too short, straining the electrical connection and causing power loss to the rear drive unit (RDU).

## Consequence

A loss of power to the rear drive unit can result in a complete loss of drive power, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Lucid will replace the wiring harness, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed December 18, 2024.  Owners may contact Lucid at 1-888-995-8243.  Lucid's number for this recall is SR-24-05-0.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153) or go to nhtsa.gov.
