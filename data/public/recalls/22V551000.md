# NHTSA recall 22V551000: Steering

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V551000 |
| Report received | 2022-07-28 |
| Manufacturer | Eldorado National- California, Inc. |
| Component | STEERING |
| Potential units affected | 14 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

ELDORADO AXESS 2021, ELDORADO AXESS 2022

## Defect

Eldorado National-California, Inc. (Eldorado National-California) is recalling certain 2021-2022 ENC Axess vehicles.  The steering gears may have been assembled incorrectly, which can cause the gears to fracture.

## Consequence

A fractured steering gear can cause a loss of steering control, increasing the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the steering gears, as necessary, free of charge.  All vehicles were remedied by December 2022.  Owners may contact Eldorado National-California customer service at 1-951-684-6868.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
