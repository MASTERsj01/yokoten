# NHTSA recall 22V704000: Engine And Engine Cooling:Engine:Oil/Lubrication: Oil Cooler

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V704000 |
| Report received | 2022-09-20 |
| Manufacturer | Ducati North America |
| Component | ENGINE AND ENGINE COOLING:ENGINE:OIL/LUBRICATION: OIL COOLER |
| Potential units affected | 1586 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

DUCATI HYPERMOTARD 939 2016, DUCATI HYPERMOTARD 939 2017, DUCATI HYPERMOTARD 939 2018, DUCATI HYPERSTRADA 939 2016, DUCATI HYPERSTRADA 939 2017, DUCATI HYPERSTRADA 939 2018

## Defect

Ducati North America (Ducati) is recalling certain 2016-2018 Hyperstrada 939, Hypermotard 939, and Hypermotard 939 SP motorcycles.  The two oil cooler hoses could contact each other and become damaged, which could cause an oil leak.

## Consequence

An oil leak could drip onto the rear tire, and increase the risk of a crash or injury.

## Remedy

Dealers will install an oil cooler hose spacer, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed October 28, 2022.  Owners may contact Ducati customer service at 1-888-391-5446.  Ducati's number for this recall is SRV-RCL-22-002.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
