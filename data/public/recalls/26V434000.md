# NHTSA recall 26V434000: Fuel System, Gasoline:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V434000 |
| Report received | 2026-07-07 |
| Manufacturer | Braun Corporation |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, GASOLINE:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 4 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

BRAUN CHEVROLET TRAVERSE 2026

## Defect

Braun Corporation (Braun) is recalling certain 2026 Chevrolet Traverse vehicles. The fuel fill line may be missing a braze connection, allowing fuel to leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak increases the risk of fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will replace the fuel fill assembly, free of charge. Owner notification letters are expected to be mailed beginning August 3, 2026.  Owners may contact Braun's customer service at 1-800-488-0359.
