# NHTSA recall 26V393000: Electrical System:Propulsion System

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 26V393000 |
| Report received | 2026-06-18 |
| Manufacturer | Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing |
| Component | ELECTRICAL SYSTEM:PROPULSION SYSTEM |
| Potential units affected | 20991 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

LEXUS RZ 2026, SUBARU SOLTERRA 2026, TOYOTA BZ 2026

## Defect

Toyota Motor Engineering & Manufacturing (Toyota) is recalling certain 2026 Toyota BZ, Lexus RZ, and Subaru Solterra battery electric vehicles. The electronic control unit (ECU) that controls the high voltage battery may experience a fault, resulting in a loss of drive power.

## Consequence

An unexpected loss of drive power increases the risk of a crash.

## Remedy

Dealers will update the battery ECU software, free of charge. Owner notification letters were mailed August 17, 2026. Owners may contact Toyota's customer service at 1-800-331-4331. Toyota's numbers for this recall are 26LA07 and 26TA11. Subaru's number for this recall is WRG26.
