# NHTSA recall 22V583000: Fuel System, Diesel:Delivery:Hoses, Lines/Piping, And Fittings

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign | 22V583000 |
| Report received | 2022-08-04 |
| Manufacturer | Newmar Corporation |
| Component | FUEL SYSTEM, DIESEL:DELIVERY:HOSES, LINES/PIPING, AND FITTINGS |
| Potential units affected | 92 |
| Recall initiated by | MFR |

## Vehicles

NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2022, NEWMAR DUTCH STAR 2023, NEWMAR ESSEX 2022, NEWMAR ESSEX 2023, NEWMAR MOUNTAIN AIRE 2022, NEWMAR MOUNTAIN AIRE 2023, NEWMAR NEW AIRE 2022, NEWMAR NEW AIRE 2023, NEWMAR SUPER STAR 2022, NEWMAR VENTANA 2022

## Defect

Newmar Corporation (Newmar) is recalling certain 2022 Super Star, Ventana, 2022-2023 New Aire, Essex, Dutch Star, and Mountain Aire motorhomes equipped with International Thermal Research powered heaters.  These heaters could have an incorrect fuel line installed that may leak.

## Consequence

A fuel leak in the presence of an ignition source can increase the risk of a fire.

## Remedy

Dealers will inspect and replace the fuel line as needed, free of charge.  Owner notification letters were mailed on September 30, 2022.  Owners may contact Newmar's customer service at 1-800-731-8300.

## Notes

Owners may also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration Vehicle Safety Hotline at 1-888-327-4236 (TTY 1-800-424-9153), or go to www.nhtsa.gov.
